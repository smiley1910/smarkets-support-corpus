Title: Forgotten password procedure

URL Source: https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212104449-Forgotten-password-procedure

Markdown Content:
If you have forgotten your password, you can request a new password by following the forgotten password procedure.

1.   On the login page, click ‘[Forgot password](https://smarkets.com/members/forgot-password)?’ This will redirect you to the password reset page.
![Image 1: Screenshot_from_2020-02-06_15-07-54.png](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/article_attachments/360006297678/Screenshot_from_2020-02-06_15-07-54.png)

2.   Once on the password reset page, you will need to enter your email address and date of birth.
![Image 2: Screenshot_from_2020-02-06_15-08-08.png](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/article_attachments/360006235797/Screenshot_from_2020-02-06_15-08-08.png)

3.   You will be sent an email with the subject: _"Reset password request from Smarkets"._ At the bottom of this email thread, there's also a link to reset your password which is in a green box. Click on it and follow the steps to reset your password.

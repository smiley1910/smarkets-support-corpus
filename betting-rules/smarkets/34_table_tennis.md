# 34. Table Tennis rules

**Smarkets [general betting rules](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212183509-1-11-General-Smarkets-betting-rules) (1-11) will apply unless superseded by the [Terms and Conditions](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/213469085-Smarkets-Terms-and-Conditions) or sports specific betting rules.**

## **34.1 General**

**34.1 If a match is incomplete all bets will be void except those which have been unconditionally determined.**

## **34.2 Types of Table Tennis Markets Offered**

34.2.1**Match Winner**: This market will be settled on which player has won.

34.2.2**Set Betting**: This market will be settled on the correct score after regular time has expired.

34.2.3**Over/Under**: This market will be settled on the sum of the points for each set played.

34.2.3**Handicap (Points):**This market will be settled on the final result and taking into consideration the handicap indicated.

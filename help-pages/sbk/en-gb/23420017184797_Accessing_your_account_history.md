You can download a CSV (comma-separated values) file of your[statement](https://smarkets.com/account/statement)page by clicking the _download as CSV'_ option located at the bottom left of the statement page screen. This allows your data to be saved in a structured, table format. Only the data displayed on the statement will be displayed.

If you wish to access more than the previous 12 months' worth of transaction history, including your entire account history,please[contact our support team](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/210638729-Contacting-Smarkets)to request this.

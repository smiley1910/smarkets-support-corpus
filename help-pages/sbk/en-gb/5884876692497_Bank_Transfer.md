## Bank transfer limits and fees

**Withdrawals**
**Fee**Free
**Minimum**£20 / €20 / $100*
**Maximum**N/A

_* Please see detailed information related to Wire Transfer in the USA [here](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360030577432-Wire-Transfer)._

## Processing time

*   Bank transfer withdrawals are processed within 5-10 business days.

## Fees

*   Smarkets do not charge you a fee for bank transfers. However, transactions might incur a fee from your bank. Please contact your bank for more info.

## Withdrawal eligibility

*   All withdrawals from SBK are subject to our [closed loop policy](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360030911631-Minimum-and-maximum-withdrawals). Where multiple deposit methods have been used you will be limited to withdrawing up to the deposited amount until net deposits are satisfied across all methods.
*   We can only process a bank transfer withdrawal when all other withdrawal options have been exhausted.
*   If your bank is in the UK, you will need to provide your account number and sort code.
*   If your bank is in the USA, you will need to provide your account number and routing number.
*   If your bank is in the Single Euro Payments Area (SEPA), other than the UK, you will need to provide your IBAN and BIC/SWIFT code.
*   If your bank is in any other country, you will need to provide your account number and sort code or equivalent bank identifier code.
*   You must upload a bank statement showing your name and the above details to verify ownership of the account.
*   Your SBK account must be verified. In this case this means you must have provided valid photo ID and your address must be verified either electronically or from a document you have provided SBK. You can find the list of accepted documents and find out how to upload documents [here.](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360030913271-Documents-required-to-verify-your-account)
*   Your SBK currency must match the currency of the bank account that you wish to withdraw to.

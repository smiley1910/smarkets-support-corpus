I've reviewed your complaint about the settlement of your accumulator at the FedEx St. Jude Championship. We're upholding that settlement, and the £5 stake returned to you is the correct and complete return on this bet.

Your bet slip identified this bet as a Bet Builder. On SBK, a Bet Builder is a multiple in which each selection is taken from the same event, as set out in Rule 9.6 of our general betting rules. All four of your Top 10 selections were markets on the same tournament, so the bet was a Bet Builder.

Under Rule 9.6.1, "If one or more selections are voided or settled as a dead heat, the whole multiple will be voided with your stake returned. This will be the case regardless of the result of other selections. This includes a case where one or more selections have already lost." Your three selections that finished T7 were settled as a dead heat, so under this rule the whole multiple was voided and your stake returned.

Golf Rule 14.3.2 states that dead heat rules apply to Top 5, 10 and 20 finish markets, and your T7 selections were settled under those rules. How a dead heat settles a multiple is set by the general rules, not by the golf rule.

Standard golf accumulators remain available, where each selection comes from a different event. A Bet Builder is specifically a multiple made up of selections from the same tournament, which is what you placed.

Before placing a bet you're responsible for being aware of the rules affecting it, including the bet type shown on your slip. The bet was placed as a Bet Builder and settled in line with its terms, so we won't be adjusting the settlement.

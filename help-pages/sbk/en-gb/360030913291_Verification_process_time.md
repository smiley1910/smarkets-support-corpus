Once you have made your first deposit the [verification process](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212634805-How-to-verify-your-account) will begin.

The automatic [electronic verification](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/210025725-Electronic-verification) will happen within minutes. If we require you to produce documentation to verify your account you will be contacted by email. When received, we aim to review all documents within 24 hours.

If you haven't received an email regarding your account verification within 24 hours, please contact our [customer support team](mailto:support@getsbk.com).

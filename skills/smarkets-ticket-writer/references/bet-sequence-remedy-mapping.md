# Bet sequence and remedy mapping

For complaints involving several bets on an original, replacement, reopened, or replacement market, build an explicit mapping before drafting. Never refer to “the bet”, “the replacement”, “these bets”, or “the amount” when more than one bet is involved.

Record each bet separately:

- Bet ID or sequence number
- stake, price, side, and market/event
- when it was placed relative to the support conversation
- original status and current status
- intended treatment: stand, void, resettle, credit, refund, or no adjustment
- reason for that treatment

Distinguish the market settlement decision from the customer remedy. A bet may be allowed to stand under the rules while a later bet is credited because support advised the customer to replace an apparently voided position. That is not necessarily a reversal of the first decision or an admission that the resettlement was contrary to Rule 4.6.

If the internal instruction is ambiguous, stop and ask for the mapping before drafting. For example, “make the user's first bet stand” and “credit each bet placed after chat” requires identifying whether the later bets are Bet 2, Bet 3, or both. Do not infer this from the order of the narrative. Do not collapse a sequence of bets into a single duplicated amount.

When the mapping is confirmed, state the outcome plainly and individually:

- Bet 1 will stand and settle against the official result.
- Bet 2 will be credited because it was placed after support advised the customer to replace the original position.
- Bet 3 will be credited for the same reason, if it falls within the approved remedy.

Then explain the operational background and the rule separately. Rule 4.6 may support correcting or resettling the original market, but it does not by itself explain a discretionary customer credit. The credit must be attributed to the support advice and the approved remedy.

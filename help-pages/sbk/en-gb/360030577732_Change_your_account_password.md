[Follow](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360030577732-Change-your-account-password/subscription.html "Opens a sign-in dialogue")

On the settings page you can change your password by following the steps below.

1. Click **My Account** at the bottom right of the page.

2. Under **Account settings**, click **Sign in details**.

![Image 2: File__4_.jpg](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/article_attachments/4409825637393/File__4_.jpg)

3. Click **Change password**.

4. Enter new password and click submit. Once updated, your new password takes effect immediately.

_Note: Your password must contain a minimum of eight characters. It must also include at least one lowercase character, one uppercase character and one number._

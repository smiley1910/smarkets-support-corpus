From the ‘Funds’ section, select ‘Deposit’.

Once on the ‘Deposit Funds’ screen select ‘Add a new payment method’ and choose your preferred payment method.

Enter the requested information and select the amount you wish to deposit.

## Limits and fees

Check the[payment methods](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360030912011-Payment-method-comparison)page for our fees, options and limits.

## Adding a deposit limit

Many customers find it useful to[set a deposit limit](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360030575152-Setting-loss-and-deposit-limits)on their accounts. This can be completed on your settings page.

## What to do if you can’t deposit

If you are unable to make a deposit it could be related to one of the following issues:

*   You have not completed your SBK account [email verification.](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360030578552-Verifying-your-email-address)

*   You're making the payment in a different country from where your card is registered.

*   Your billing address is different to the address registered to your SBK account.

*   You have used your card too many times in the last 12 hours.

*   Your bank has blocked the payment as a security measure.

*   You have insufficient funds in your bank/e-wallet account.

*   Your Skrill or Neteller account needs to be verified.

*   The [deposit limit](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360030575152-Setting-loss-and-deposit-limits)has been reached for one transaction.

If you're not sure which issue has caused your deposit to fail, please[contact our support team](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360030913931-Contacting-SBK)and we'll be happy to help.

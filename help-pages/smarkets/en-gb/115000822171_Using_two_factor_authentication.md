## What is two-factor authentication?

Two-factor authentication (2FA) is a way of making your account more secure. In addition to entering your regular email address and password, with 2FA enabled you will also be required to enter a one-time password which is generated by a third-party application on your mobile device. This greatly reduces the risk of your account being compromised as each one-time password is randomly generated and lasts only 30 seconds.

## How to activate

*   Visit the desktop site.

*   To turn on 2FA click on the ‘Settings’ tab and select ‘[Login](https://smarkets.com/account/settings/login)’. From here, select ‘Enable two-factor authentication’. 

![Image 1: Screenshot_from_2020-03-31_14-38-00.png](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/article_attachments/360007373237/Screenshot_from_2020-03-31_14-38-00.png)

*   Follow the instructions on the pop-up window. You will need to download one of the following apps on your Smartphone:
    *   Android, iOS, and Blackberry: Google Authenticator
    *   Android and iOS: Duo Mobile
    *   Windows Phone: Authenticator

*   Scan the barcode with your app and enter the one-time password generated by the app.

## How to log in using 2FA

*   Enter your email address and password as normal. 
*   Select ‘Sign In’. A pop-up will then appear prompting you to verify your identity.
*   Open the third-party authentication app on your mobile device.
*   Enter the one-time verification code as it appears on the app.

## How to turn off 2FA

*   Visit the desktop site.
*   To disable 2 factor-authentication simply click on the ‘Settings’ tab and select ‘Login’.
*   Click “Disable”.

## How to use 2FA if you no longer have access to your chosen mobile device

If you recently purchased a new phone or if your phone was lost/stolen please send us your Proof of ID to support@smarkets.com from your Smarkets email address, so that our support team will be able to disable this for you.

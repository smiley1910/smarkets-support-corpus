# Customer Policy FAQ — Common Non-API Questions

## Closed Loop Payments

### What It Is (Layman's)

Money in and money out must follow the same path. Your deposit comes from your bank account or card → winnings must go back to that exact same account or card. No detours. No sending to a friend. No withdrawing to a different card.

### Why It's Required

This is an anti-money-laundering (AML) rule. Without closed loop:

1. Person A deposits €10,000 from a stolen or suspicious source
2. Places a few small bets to look legitimate
3. Withdraws the €9,800 remainder to Person B's account
4. Money is now "clean" and untraceable to the original crime

Closed loop closes that gap — the money has to return to the same verified source it came from. Gambling sites are legally required to enforce this under their operating licence (MGA, UKGC, etc.). It is not optional. It is not the site being difficult. If they didn't enforce it, they would lose their licence.

### Smarkets T&Cs Reference

- **Section 5.1:** "[...] The name on the card or the bank account or any other payment method should be identical to that of the account holder."
- **Section 5.1:** "If Smarkets identifies that funds have been deposited to your account from a third party, we reserve the right to immediately return the deposit back to the source and void any winnings that have arisen from such a deposit."
- **Section 5.9:** "Funds cannot be transferred from the account of one account holder to the account of another account holder."
- **Section 5.15:** "All deposits, transfers and withdrawals will be checked to prevent money laundering."

### What the Customer May Say

- *"Why can't I withdraw to my girlfriend's account? She has a different bank."*
- *"This is my money, I should be able to send it wherever I want."*
- *"I lost my card, can I withdraw to a different one?"*
- *"Bet365 let me do it, why can't Smarkets?"*
- *"It's only €20, why does it matter?"*

## Account Sharing

### Policy (Exact Clauses)

Smarkets T&Cs are explicit — account sharing is prohibited by multiple overlapping clauses:

| Clause | What It Says |
|--------|-------------|
| **2.8** | *"You may only open and maintain one account, and this account must not be shared with any other individual. As a result you may only open one account per address, IP address or device."* |
| **2.14** | *"You must keep your login credentials secret at all times, and you remain solely responsible for the security and confidentiality of your account."* |
| **2.4** | *"You are prohibited from selling, transferring and/or acquiring accounts for or from other account holders."* |
| **6.32** | *"You are forbidden from placing bets with the intention of transferring funds to another account."* |

### Why It's Enforced Heavily

It's not Smarkets being difficult — it's AML and licensing law. The closed-loop rule (money in → same person out) only works if the person on the account is the one using it. An account shared with a friend breaks that chain entirely. If a regulator found Smarkets knowingly allowed account sharing, the company would risk its licence.

### What Happens If Caught

Winnings can be voided, account closed, funds held pending investigation — and flagged to the MGA if money laundering is suspected.

### What the Customer May Say

- *"Can my friend use my account to place a bet while I'm busy?"*
- *"I'm letting my brother use my account, is that okay?"*
- *"We're a syndicate, we share one account for convenience."*
- *"I just shared my login with my wife so she could check the balance."*
- *"Why does it matter who uses the account? The money is mine."*
- *"It's my account, I can do what I want with it."*

## **Full T&C’s**

1. In order to be eligible for this promotion you will need to first opt in in the ‘Promotions’ section in your SBK account.

2. You must then place £50 worth of bets at odds of 2/1 (3.0) or greater on any market between **08:00 BST Monday and 23:59 BST Sunday each week.**You will then be eligible to receive one £5 Free Bet token to use on a Horse Racing Multiple (min 5 legs) and one £5 Free Football Bet Builder token (min 5 legs). Free bets will be credited to your promotional balance once £50 worth of bets have been settled.

3. Bets placed prior to opting in do not count towards this promotion.

4. Your 1 x £5 Free Bet token for a Horse Racing Multiple and 1 x £5 Free Football Bet Builder Token will be credited to your promotional balance upon settlement of the qualifying bet and have a minimum 5 leg requirement.

5. Free Bets are not eligible for use on Full Cover bets.

6. Bets must be placed and settled before **23:59 BST Sunday each week.**

7. Any bets placed must have minimum odds of 2/1 (3.0).

8. Multiples bets will contribute towards this promotion as long as the cumulative odds are greater than 2/1 (3.0).

9. Each way bets will act as two separate bets when contributing towards this promotion. Only bets that are equal to or greater than 2/1 (3.0) will contribute towards this promotion. Example: A €5 each way bet at 4/1 (5.00) to win; 4/5 (1.8) to place; will contribute €10 towards the promotion. A €5 each way bet at 2/1 (3.00) to win; 1/2 (1.5) to place; will contribute €5 towards the promotion.

10. Bets that are cashed out, voided or placed using free bets do not count towards this promotion.

11. Bets placed to meet the requirements of other promotional offers do not count towards this promotion.

12. Only real money bets count towards this promotion. Free Bets and Bet Bundle tokens do not count towards this promotion.

13. This offer is available to existing SBK customers in UK, Ireland & Malta.

14. This offer is strictly limited to one per individual, family, household address, email address, same payment account number, and shared computer.

15. SBK reserves the right in its discretion to exclude, withdraw and/or limit the availability of the offer to certain customers.

16. Free Bets will appear in your promotional balance, have no monetary value and are non-withdrawable.

17. Free Bets can only be placed on their specified sport and market type.

18. Free Bets that have not been used will expire 7 days after they have been credited to your promotional balance.

19. SBK standard terms for promotions apply.

20. This offer is only available to SBK users, to use on SBK; not Smarkets.

21. This offer is only available to customers in countries where you are able to operate an SBK account.

22. Please gamble responsibly, 18+.[BeGambleAware.org](http://begambleaware.org/).

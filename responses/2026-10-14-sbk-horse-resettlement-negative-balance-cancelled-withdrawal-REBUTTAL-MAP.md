- Product: SBK sportsbook.
- Jurisdiction: UKGC (UK). ADR route: IBAS (T&Cs 16.2).
- Event / Bets: Two horse racing bets [BET ID 1] and [BET ID 2] on [RACE / SELECTION].
- Initial settlement: Settled as winners initially based on first past the post result, showing €442.50 in account balance.
- Withdrawal request: Customer initiated a withdrawal for €442.50 while the settlement was provisional.
- Official correction: Official result confirmed following [stewards enquiry]. Under Horse Racing Rule 13.1.1, the weighed-in result is the official result for settlement.
- Resettlement: Markets resettled under General Rules 4.6 and 4.7 to reflect the official outcome. The provisional winnings of €442.50 were reversed.

## Customer claim-by-claim rebuttal map

### CUSTOMER POINT 1 — "I am writing to make a formal complaint regarding a negative balance of €442.50 that has been placed on my account following the resettlement of two horse racing bets... I am now being told that I must deposit €442.50 to bring my account back to zero."
Draft response: Para 2 ("To clarify your current account position, your balance is zero and no deposit is required.") and Para 4.
Reasoning: Directly addresses the customer's primary premise and financial anxiety. Clarifies that there is no negative balance on the account and no payment is owed, because the pending withdrawal was cancelled to offset the resettlement adjustment.

### CUSTOMER POINT 2 — "I am therefore asking SBK to formally review: 1. The original settlement of the two bets. 2. The subsequent resettlement and exactly why the €442.50 negative balance was created. 3. The timing of the settlement, withdrawal and subsequent resettlement."
Draft response: Para 3 and Para 4.
Reasoning: Explains the sequence factually: initial settlement was based on first past the post; official result followed [stewards enquiry]; weighed-in result governs under Rule 13.1.1; Rules 4.6 and 4.7 reserve the right to reverse incorrect settlement and adjust balances. Reversing the winnings initially created a negative balance while the withdrawal was pending, but cancelling the pending withdrawal cleared that negative balance to zero.

### CUSTOMER POINT 3 — "4. Whether I was given any indication that the winnings were provisional or subject to resettlement before I withdrew the funds."
Draft response: Para 3 and Para 4.
Reasoning: Anchored in published rules (Rule 13.1.1 on weighed-in official results and Rules 4.6/4.7 on balance amendments). We explain the operation of the rules on the account without moralising or lecturing about whether the customer should have known.

### CUSTOMER POINT 4 — "5. Whether SBK can offer a goodwill resolution, partial waiver or other reasonable arrangement given the circumstances and my history as a customer."
Draft response: Para 5 ("As the bets settled as losses under the official weighed-in result and the funds remained on the platform, no goodwill payment or balance adjustment will be made. The settlement stands.").
Reasoning: No waiver is needed because there is no negative balance to waive. No goodwill payout of the provisional winnings is warranted because the bets lost under the official governing body result and no funds left the account.

### CUSTOMER POINT 5 — "I cannot afford to deposit €442.50 at present, so I would appreciate it if no further pressure is placed on me to make that payment while my formal complaint is being reviewed."
Draft response: Para 2.
Reasoning: Directly resolved: balance is zero, no payment is owed, no collection pressure applies.

### CUSTOMER POINT 6 — "Please acknowledge this as a formal complaint and provide me with your response following your investigation."
Draft response: Para 1 (acknowledgement of formal complaint review) and Para 6 (IBAS ADR route per T&Cs 16.2 for final response on formal complaint).
Reasoning: Handled as formal complaint response under UKGC jurisdiction. Verbatim IBAS contact details provided.

## Deliberately NOT responded to / not engaged

- Customer's praise / loyalty statements ("regular and avid SBK customer", "genuinely enjoy using SBK"). Stating appreciation or flattering the customer back distracts from the factual resolution.
- Customer's statement regarding live chat agent authority ("I appreciate that the live chat agent may not have the authority to change the balance"). Not engaged; facts of the account take precedence.
- Customer's offer of screenshots ("I have screenshots of the bets... and can provide these if required"). Unnecessary to request as account records already confirm the bets and transactions.

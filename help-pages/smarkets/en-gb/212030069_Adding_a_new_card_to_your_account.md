## How can we help you?

[Help Centre](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb)[Safer Gambling](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/categories/360001480391)[Contact Us](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/categories/360001480451)

You can add a new debit card by making a deposit to your Smarkets account.

1. Select the deposit icon on the right hand side of the site or [click here](https://smarkets.com/account/deposit/)to go directly to the deposit page.

2.Once on the deposit screen select 'Debit Card' and click 'Add new card'.

3. Enter the requested information, select the amount you wish to deposit and click 'Start Deposit'.

4. Once the payment is processed you’ll be returned to Smarkets. The card details have now been saved to your account.

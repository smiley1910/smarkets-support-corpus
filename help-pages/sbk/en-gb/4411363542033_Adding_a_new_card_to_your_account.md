Title: Adding a new card to your account

URL Source: https://help.getsbk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/4411363542033-Adding-a-new-card-to-your-account

Markdown Content:
[Follow](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/4411363542033-Adding-a-new-card-to-your-account/subscription.html "Opens a sign-in dialogue")

You can add a new debit card by making a deposit to your SBK account.

From the ‘Funds’ section, select ‘Deposit’.

Once on the ‘Deposit Funds’ screen select ‘Add a new payment method’ and choose the Debit card option.

Enter the requested information and select the amount you wish to deposit.

Once the deposit has been made, your card details will be saved to your account.

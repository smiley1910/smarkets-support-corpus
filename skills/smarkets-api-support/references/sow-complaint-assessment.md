# Source of Wealth Complaint Assessment

Framework for assessing customer complaints about SoW/SoF processing delays. Created July 2026 after a correction: the 24-hour document review SLA was incorrectly applied to a SoW complaint.

## Critical Distinction: Two Separate Categories

### Account Verification (HAS a documented SLA)
- **Help article**: "Verification process time" (ID: 212062349)
- **Category**: Account Verification
- **Covers**: Electronic verification, ID documents, residency proof
- **Documented SLA**: "we aim to review all documents within 24 hours"
- **Electronic verification**: "within minutes"

### Source of Funds & Wealth (NO documented SLA)
- **Help articles**: 
  - "Explaining source of funds and wealth checks" (Smarkets: 16426398858781, SBK: 16491338515613)
  - "Asking about how you fund your account" (Smarkets: 16426627719581, SBK: 16491357148061)
  - "Account restrictions" (Smarkets: 16427904326685, SBK: 16491450442269)
- **Category**: Source of Funds & Wealth
- **Documented SLA**: NONE. Only "reasonable timeframe" and "required timeframe" — never defined with a number.
- **T&Cs**: Clause 2.18 (Smarkets) / 2.19 (SBK) — "Should you fail to provide satisfactory responses to our queries, Smarkets may suspend the account..."

## What the SoW Docs Actually Say

All three SoW help articles use identical language:
> "You are not obligated to provide the requested information but if we are unable to complete the verification process within a reasonable timeframe we are required to restrict or suspend your account."

Account restrictions article adds:
> "If we are unable to contact you or you do not complete the verification process within the required timeframe, it may be necessary for us to restrict or suspend your account."

Key points:
- No specific number (days/hours) is ever given
- No SLA for how quickly Smarkets must reach out after a trigger
- No deadline for the customer to respond
- Restrictions escalate: deposit block → bet block → temporary suspension
- If docs provided later, account gets reviewed again

## Complaint Assessment Framework

### "No SLA was breached" — DEFENSIBLE
If a complaint cites a specific timeframe Smarkets missed for SoW, this is defensible. There is no documented SoW SLA to breach. The 24-hour aim is for Account Verification only.

### "The complaint is not valid" — STRONGER CLAIM, use with caution
"No SLA breached" ≠ "complaint has no merit." Two separate things:
1. **SLA breach claim**: Not valid — no SoW SLA exists to breach.
2. **Overall handling/reasonableness claim**: May still have merit even without an SLA. "Reasonable timeframe" is an interpretable standard — a regulator (UKGC) or ADR provider (IBAS) can assess it independently.

### Factors that weaken the "not valid" position:
- **Customer acted promptly** (submitted docs, responded to queries quickly) — no failure on their side
- **Smarkets was the bottleneck** — long silence after receiving everything requested
- **Automated suspension emails** sent to a customer waiting on Smarkets — this is a process problem separate from SLAs. Sending "get in touch or we'll suspend" to someone who already responded is misleading.
- **"Reasonable timeframe" obligation is on both sides** — the docs say verification must be completed within a reasonable timeframe; Smarkets has an obligation to process promptly too.

## NEVER Do This
- Do NOT cite the 24-hour document review aim from "Verification process time" when assessing a SoW complaint. That SLA is for Account Verification (ID/residency), a different process and help centre category.
- Do NOT assume "no SLA = complaint invalid." The absence of a number makes it harder to challenge but doesn't make it automatically unchallengeable.

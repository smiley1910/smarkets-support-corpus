## **35.1 General**

**‘**Gaelic Games’ applies to both ‘Gaelic Football’ & ‘Hurling’ sports.****

Gaelic Games markets apply to the standard length of 70 minutes including stoppage/injury time unless stated otherwise. Markets for standard 70-minute games do not take extra-time or penalty shootouts into account.

## **35.2****Types of Gaelic Games Markets Offered**

35.2.1 **Full-time result**: This market will be settled on which team has won after regular time has expired or if there is a draw.

35.2.2 **Handicap Betting:**This market will be settled on the final result and taking into consideration the handicap indicated.

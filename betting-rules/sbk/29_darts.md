## **29.1 General**

29.1 The player progressing to the next round will be deemed the winner, providing one of the players has thrown a dart at the start of the first leg. If the first dart is not thrown, all bets will be voided.

29.2 Where a match is uncompleted all bets on handicap markets are voided unless the market has been unconditionally determined.

29.3 Correct score markets will be settled on the correct score at the end of the match. If the match starts, but is not completed, correct score bets will be made void.

29.4 If a player in a tournament fails to start, all bets relating to that player in that tournament will be voided. If the player starts off but is later disqualified or withdraws, they will be settled as a loser.

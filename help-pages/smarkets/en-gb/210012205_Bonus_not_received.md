# Bonus not received

You must sign up with the promo code '**COMMFREE**' to benefit from the welcome bonus.

An initial deposit of £10 (or currency equivalent) must be made by card or Instant Banking to qualify for the welcome bonus.

The welcome bonus is limited to one per individual, family, household address, email address, same payment account number, and shared computer.

For more information about the qualifying deposits and bonus for each currency please review the [terms of welcome bonus.](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/12498043366685-Trade-with-0-commission-at-Smarkets-)

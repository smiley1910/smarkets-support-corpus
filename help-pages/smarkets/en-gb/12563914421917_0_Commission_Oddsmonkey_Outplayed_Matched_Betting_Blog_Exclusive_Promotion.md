Title: 0% Commission - Oddsmonkey / Outplayed / Matched Betting Blog Exclusive Promotion

URL Source: https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/12563914421917-0-Commission-Oddsmonkey-Outplayed-Matched-Betting-Blog-Exclusive-Promotion

Markdown Content:
1. This offer will run from 16:30 GMT on Thursday, January 30th, 2025 onwards and is available to Smarkets matched bettors who are premium OddsMonkey, Outplayed or Matched Betting Blog members and who first [**opt in.**](https://smarkets.com/promotions/zero-percent/)

2. This offer cannot be used in conjunction with Skrill, Neteller, Trustly or Paypal deposits.

3. To be eligible for the offer, OddsMonkey, Outplayed or Matched Betting Blog members must complete the following steps in this order:

i)**First, [opt in](https://smarkets.com/promotions/zero-percent/)**with their Smarkets account email address through the relevant Smarkets landing page and receive the confirmation email from Smarkets confirming their account has successfully opted-in.

ii) following opt-in, use an OddsMonkey, Outplayed or Matched Betting Blog integration to place a bet.

iii) after placing the first bet, use an OddsMonkey, Outplayed or Matched Betting Blog integration to place a bet at least once every 30 days.

iv) have not received notice from Smarkets that their account is ineligible for the promotion.

4. 0% commission will be activated within 15 minutes of placing the qualifying integrated bet. This bet will be settled at the commission rate active on the account at the time of settlement.

5. Smarkets reserves the right to revoke eligibility for the promotion for individual accounts. This will be done by way of an email notification stating that no further extension can be made to the eligibility period. Any bets which settle after the expiry of the eligibility period will settle at standard commission rates.

6. While a customer is eligible, 0% commission will be applied to all bets that settle during the promotional period. Skrill, Neteller, Trustly and Paypal will be made unavailable as deposit methods for the duration of the promotional period.

7. Customers who access Smarkets through any non-OddsMonkey / Outplayed / Matched Betting Blog API or trading software will be ineligible for this offer.

8. This offer is only available to customers in countries where you are able to operate a Smarkets account, excluding Sweden.

9. By opting into the promotion, or placing a bet through the Smarkets integration, you agree to allow Smarkets and OddsMonkey / Outplayed / Matched Betting Blog to share data in order to establish your eligibility for the offer.

10. [Smarkets T&Cs](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/213469085-Smarkets-Terms-and-Conditions)apply.

Please contact customer support either via live chat or email: [support@smarkets.com,](mailto:support@smarkets.com) should you have any issues integrating your account after following the steps stipulated above in that exact order.

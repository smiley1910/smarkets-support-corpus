To remove a card from your SBK account you will need to go to Funds, situated at the bottom of the screen, then select Deposit. Select a card and then click on the ‘EDIT’ button at the top right hand side of the screen. From here you can go on the card you wish to remove and you will simply need to tap on the red button to the left of the card.
Below are some screenshots showing all steps on how to remove a card.

![Image 2: Screenshot_20230517-094626.jpg](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/article_attachments/11020535349789)![Image 3: Screenshot_20230517-094641.jpg](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/article_attachments/11020617919901)![Image 4: Screenshot_20230517-094647.jpg](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/article_attachments/11020942271005)![Image 5: Screenshot_20230517-094655.jpg](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/article_attachments/11020984856349)

If you can't remove your card, please contact our[support team.](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360030913931-Contacting-SBK)We may be able to remove the card for you however, we can only remove bank cards if certain conditions are met and may require verification documents to remove your card.

Replacing your card

If you're adding a replacement card, please remove your old card once the replacement has been added by making a deposit.

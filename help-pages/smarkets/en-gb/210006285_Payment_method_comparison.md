_Notes:_

*   _For instant free deposits and unlimited withdrawals we strongly recommend using a Visa debit card as your payment option._
*   _If customers withdraw more than 4 times in a 7 day period from an e-wallet payment method, the fourth and further withdrawals will take 24hr to process._
*   _Smarkets do not charge you transaction fees. However, transactions might incur a fee from your bank/e-wallet. Please contact your provider for more info._

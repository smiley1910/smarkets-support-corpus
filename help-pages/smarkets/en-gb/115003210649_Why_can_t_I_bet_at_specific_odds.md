Title: Why can't I bet at specific odds?

URL Source: https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/115003210649-Why-can-t-I-bet-at-specific-odds

Markdown Content:
Our underlying system matches bets between customers in percentage prices. If a user is displaying the odds in decimal form, we round the percentage prices to two decimal places, as displayed on the exchange. So although you’re backing at 1.65, the percentage price would in fact be 60.61%.

Tick size is the minimum price movement.The price movements vary with the tick size representing the minimum incremental price movement that can be experienced on the exchange.

This can lead to instances where a user may want to bet at a specific price, but will not be able to. For instance if you wanted to bet at 6.1 our system would only allow you to bet at 6 or 6.2.

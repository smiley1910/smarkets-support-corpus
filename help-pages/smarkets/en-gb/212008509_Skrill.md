## About Skrill

Skrill is an e-wallet which provides a fast, simple and secure way of transferring money to your Smarkets account. Skrill offer several deposit options according to your country of residence.

To learn more about Skrill or to open a Skrill account, please go to their [website](https://www.skrill.com/en/).

## Processing time

*   Skrill deposits are processed within a few minutes.
*   Skrill withdrawals are processed within 1-2 business days.

## Fees

*   Smarkets absorb both deposit and withdrawal fees when using Skrill.

## Additional information

Accounts

*   You can only register one Skrill account with Smarkets.

Deposits

*   Smarkets will only accept deposits in the specific currency you have designated for your Smarkets account.

Withdrawals

*   You can only make a withdrawal to your Skrill account once you have made a deposit via Skrill. Your Skrill account will then appear on the withdrawal page.
*   All withdrawals from Smarkets are subject to our [closed loop policy](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212620285-Withdrawal-limits). Where multiple deposit methods have been used you will be limited to withdrawing up to the deposited amount until net deposits are satisfied across all methods.

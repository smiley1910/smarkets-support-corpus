# iOS and Android mobile application

![Image 3: hd-hero-mobile.png](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/article_attachments/360001926511/hd-hero-mobile.png)

We have launched our first dedicated trading app on both iOS and Android. Download, sign in and start trading on one of the fastest, most secure and user-friendly betting apps.

The mobile app allows users to:

*   Manage your position on the go with [keep bets in-play](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/214511865-Keep-bets-in-play) and [trade out](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/214274825-Trade-out-button-on-Smarkets) features
*   Trade sports, politics or current affairs with accurate, reliable and real-time prices
*   Bet with full exchange functionality
*   Find trades quickly with our unique search tool
*   Choose whether to trade with either the light or dark interface

You can quickly view the status of any bet in the ‘Open Bets’ tab. Depending on which bets you have placed, you may see tabs that allow you to show only specific bet types:

*   **LIVE:** Bets you have placed on events currently in-play
*   **Upcoming:**Bets you have placed on events that are not yet in-play
*   **Multiples:** Doubles, Trebles, Four-folds, etc.
*   **Outrights:** Bets you have placed on long-term markets

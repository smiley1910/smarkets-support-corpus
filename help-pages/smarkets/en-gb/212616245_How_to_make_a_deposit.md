Title: How to make a deposit

URL Source: https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212616245-How-to-make-a-deposit

Markdown Content:
Follow the step-by-step guide below to help you make a deposit on Smarkets:

1.   Click your balance and then select[deposit](https://smarkets.com/account/deposit).
2.   Once on the deposit screen select a payment method.![Image 1: Screenshot_from_2020-04-01_09-30-16.png](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/article_attachments/360007387437/Screenshot_from_2020-04-01_09-30-16.png)
3.   Follow the on-screen instructions to complete your deposit.

## Limits and fees

Check the [payment methods](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/210006285-Payment-method-comparison) page for our fees, options and limits.

## Adding a deposit limit

Many customers find it useful to [set a deposit limit](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212020709-Setting-a-deposit-limit) on their account. This can be completed on your settings page.

## What to do if you can’t deposit

If you are unable to make a deposit it could be related to one of the following issues:

*   You have not completed your Smarkets account[email verification](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212060309-Verifying-your-email-address).
*   You're making the payment in a different country to where your card is registered.
*   Your billing address is different to the address registered to your Smarkets account.
*   You have used your card too many times in the last 12 hours.
*   Your bank has blocked the payment as a security measure.
*   You have insufficient funds in your bank/e-wallet account.
*   Your Skrill or Neteller account needs to be verified.
*   The[deposit limit](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212020209-Deposit-limits)has been reached for one transaction.

If you're not sure which issue has caused your deposit to fail, please[contact our support team](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/210638729-Contacting-Smarkets)and we'll be happy to help.

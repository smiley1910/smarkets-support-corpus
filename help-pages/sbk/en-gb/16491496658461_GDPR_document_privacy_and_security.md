## **What is GDPR?**

General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is an EU law with mandatory rules for how companies and organisations, such as SBK, use personal data. It unites legislation across the European Union and came into effect on 25/05/2018. The core focuses of GDPR are data governance, data management and data transparency.

## **What does GDPR mean for me?**

We will only use or process your personal information in line with the terms and conditions you have agreed to or as specified within our privacy policy.

We will never keep your data longer than is necessary for the purpose which it was requested for. The only exception to this is where necessary to comply with legal and licensing obligations but no further action will be required from you.

## **Is my information safe?**

Document and data security is of the utmost importance so to protect all customer information against theft or misuse we use stringent technical and organisational measures to protect against such threats.

## **I want you to delete my personal data!**

If you decide to stop using our service, we will delete your data after a certain amount of time as outlined in our terms and conditions. This time period is dependent upon the country and licence your account is registered to.

For further information on or your rights, please refer to our [privacy policy](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360030912451-Privacy-Policy).

# Commission breakdown

At Smarkets we charge 2% commission on net market winnings for customers on the[Standard Tier](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212654665-Smarkets-commission-FAQ). This means if you make a net loss on a market you will not be charged commission.

Some customers pay different commission based on their accounts hitting certain limits. You can read about out different commission tiers[here.](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212654665-Smarkets-commission-FAQ)

You can view the commission paid for each settlement at the account page.

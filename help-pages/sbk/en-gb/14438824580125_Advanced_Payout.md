SBK’s Advanced Payout feature allows you to receive any return from your [full cover bets](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/14436160505245) quicker than you would anywhere else.

**How this feature works**

When you place any [full cover bet](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/14436160505245), you will receive any profit from the individual legs as soon as possible.

For example, when you place a lucky 15, consisting of 4 singles, 6 doubles, 4 trebles and 1 four-fold, rather than the traditional sportsbooks settling all of these bets when the last leg is complete your bet will start to settle the moment the result of the first selection is settled.

![Image 2: Bet Slip - Full Covers.png](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/article_attachments/14634425157789)

In our example, as soon as the 12:20 race has finished, if Belles Benefit has won we’ll settle the first single straight away and if Banteer then goes on to win the 13:20 race you’ll be credited with the return from your double immediately as well.

In Your Bets you’ll be able to see the running return you have made next to each bet you have placed to keep track of what you’ve received so far and the potential returns from the remaining selection(s).

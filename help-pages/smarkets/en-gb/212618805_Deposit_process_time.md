# Deposit process time

Deposit process times vary depending on your chosen deposit method. The table below explains the deposit process time for each payment option:

**Payment option****Time**
[Instant Bank Transfer](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360006230237)Instant
[Visa debit card](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212009749-Visa-Debit)Instant
[Solo / Maestro card](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212607305-Maestro)Instant
[MasterCard / Eurocard](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212608105-Visa-Mastercard-Eurocard)Instant
[Neteller](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212008689-NETELLER)Instant
[Skrill](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212008509-Skrill)Instant
[Bank Transfer](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212009689-Bank-Transfer)3-5 business days
[Trustly](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360003024392)Instant
[PayPal](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360019121771-PayPal)Instant

_Note: If your deposit has not reached your account please ensure the deposit was complete and the funds debited from your account. If there is still an issue please contact \_[support@smarkets.com](mailto:support@smarkets.com).\__

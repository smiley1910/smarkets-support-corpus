## **What is two-factor authentication?**

Two-factor authentication (2FA) is a way of making your account more secure. In addition to entering your regular email address and password, with 2FA enabled you will also be required to enter a one-time password which is generated by a third party application on your mobile device. This greatly reduces the risk of your account being compromised as each one-time password is randomly generated and lasts only 30 seconds.

## **How to activate**

*   In the SBK app, navigate to the Account tab, and then to 'Sign in details'.

*   To turn on 2FA, tap the ‘Enable two-factor authentication’ button.

![Image 2: File__2_.jpg](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/article_attachments/4409833123857/File__2_.jpg)

*   Follow the instructions that appear. You will need to download one of the following apps on your Android or iOS device:
    *   Authy
    *   Duo Mobile
    *   Google Authenticator

*   Paste the code noted in Step 2 from SBK into your authentication app of choice.
*   Paste the generated 2FA code from your authentication app into SBK.

## **How to login using 2FA**

*   Sign into SBK as normal. 
*   You will then see a 2FA code entry screen.
*   Open the third party authentication app on your mobile device and generate a code for SBK.
*   Enter the one time verification code as it appears on the app.

## **How to turn off 2FA**

*   In the SBK app, navigate to the Account tab, and then to 'Sign in details'

*   To turn off 2FA, tap the ‘Disable two-factor authentication’ button.

## **How to use 2FA if you no longer have access to your chosen mobile device**

If you recently purchased a new phone or if your phone was lost/stolen please send us an email to [support@getsbk.com](mailto:support@getsbk.com) from your SBK email address. Please attach a proof of identity document such as a passport, identity card or driver's license so our support team can disable your 2FA for you.

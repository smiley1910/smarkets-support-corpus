Title: Support organisations

URL Source: https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212653665-Support-organisations

Markdown Content:
For advice and support in relation to your gambling, or if you are concerned about a friend or family member then there are numerous support services that can offer professional and dedicated help:

## **GamCare**

GamCare provides support for the prevention and treatment of gambling addiction. They offer support and services through chatrooms, a national helpline, and both online and face-to-face counseling.

Contact details:

_Phone:_ _0808 8020 133_

_Website: [http://www.gamcare.org.uk](http://www.gamcare.org.uk/)_

## **Gamblers Anonymous**

Gamblers Anonymous or ‘GA’ are a group of people who have joined together to tackle their own gambling problems and help others who suffer from gambling addiction do the same.

Contact details:

_UK website: [http://www.gamblersanonymous.org.uk](http://www.gamblersanonymous.org.uk/)_

_International website: [http://www.gamblersanonymous.org](http://www.gamblersanonymous.org/)_

## **Gambling Therapy (Gordon Moody)**

Contact details:

_Website:[https://www.gamblingtherapy.org/en](https://www.gamblingtherapy.org/en)_

## **National Problem Gambling Clinic**

The National Problem Gambling Clinic treats problem gamblers in England and Wales. It also offers advice for family members who may be affected by someone's gambling and assist with financial, employment, social, and relationship difficulties.

Contact details:

_Phone:_ _020 7381 7722_

_Website: [https://www.cnwl.nhs.uk/services/mental-health-services/addictions-and-substance-misuse/national-problem-gambling-clinic](https://www.cnwl.nhs.uk/services/mental-health-services/addictions-and-substance-misuse/national-problem-gambling-clinic)_

## **Big Deal**

Big Deal, operated by GamCare, is targeted primarily at supporting and educating those who feel that they may have an issue with underage gambling.

Contact details:

_Phone:_ _0808 8020 133_

_Website:[https://www.bigdeal.org.uk/](https://www.bigdeal.org.uk/)_

## **Samaritans**

Samaritans offer professional round-the-clock support for people who have feelings of suicide.

Contact details:

_Phone:_ _116 123_

_Website:[https://www.samaritans.org/](https://www.samaritans.org/)_

Title: How to use the document uploader

URL Source: https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212061789-How-to-use-the-document-uploader

Markdown Content:
If you’re required to provide [documents to verify your account](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212634845-Documents-required-to-verify-your-account), you can use the document uploader. This link will have been sent to you via the verifying your account email.

Simply drag and drop your files in the space provided or choose your file.

Click[here](https://smarkets.com/account/file-upload/)to go straight to the document uploader.

![Image 1: Screenshot_from_2018-09-25_11-02-18.png](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/article_attachments/360012062492/Screenshot_from_2018-09-25_11-02-18.png)

_Note: Files larger than 5MB in size will not be accepted so please ensure they comply with this before you start the process. If you have any questions please contact [support@smarkets.com](mailto:support@smarkets.com)._

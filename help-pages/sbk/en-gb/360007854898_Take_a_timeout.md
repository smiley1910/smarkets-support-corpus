## **What is a time out?**

A time out allows you to take a short break from gambling, enabling you to block access to your account for a period of 1, 7, 31 or 42 days.

During this period you will be completely unable to access your account or any of its features*. At the end of your selected time out period your account will automatically be reopened.

**_Note: You will be unable to reverse the ‘time out’ during the set period._**

_*This includes the ability to request withdrawals via your account cashier. Should you wish to make a withdrawal during a time-out period, you can contact our support team via email who will be more than happy to assist you._

## **How do I take a timeout?**

1. A time out can be set by logging into your SBK account and accessing the 'My limits' page.

2. From here, click on the 'Time out' option.

3. You can then configure and select the duration you wish to take a break for.

4. Once configured, you are prompted to confirm and then 'Initiate Time Out'

# Complaints Writer — Playbook & Precedent Scenarios

## 1. Resettlement, Negative Balance & Cancelled Withdrawals

### Operational Mechanics
- **Scenario:** A market initially settles on a provisional result (e.g. first past the post). The customer requests a withdrawal of the winnings. Following an official result correction (e.g. stewards enquiry), the market is resettled on the official result.
- **Rules:** Rule 13.1.1 (weighed in result is the official result for settlement); General Rules 4.6 (right to reverse incorrect settlements), 4.7 (amend customer balances to reflect corrected settlement), and 5.2 (right to recover funds disbursed in error).
- **The Negative Balance Explanation:** Reversing provisional winnings initially places the ledger into a debit while the withdrawal is pending. When SBK cancels the pending withdrawal, those funds return to the ledger, fully clearing the negative balance to zero.
- **The Position:** The account balance is zero; no deposit is required; the bets lost under the official result and settlement stands.

## 2. Void and Reinstate Variants (Never Merge)

### Variant 1: Denial (No Credit)
- **Position:** Original bet was voided in error or subject to correction, and void reversal / settlement on official result was correct under Rules 4.6 and 9.4. Customer argues void was permanent.
- **Authority:** The only voids made permanent by rule are 8.3.5 (Instant Match pending confirmation) and 12.4 (3-hour match abandonment). General voids do not carry an irrevocable status.

### Variant 2: Partial Grant (Goodwill Credit for Chat Advice)
- **Position:** Original bet settlement is UPHELD under published rules. Re-bet liability is CREDITED as a discretionary goodwill gesture under Rule 9.4 because the customer re-bet in reliance on explicit incorrect live chat guidance.
- **Phasing:** Keep the rule-backed limb (settlement stands) separate from the discretionary limb (goodwill credit). Use the exact phrase "good faith" and label the credit as a "goodwill adjustment". Include the complaints pointer.

### Variant 3: Full Grant
- **Position:** Both positions credited with exact bet IDs and amounts. Authorised only where explicit incorrect guidance occurred. Include a stern forward-looking notice that settlement corrections are permitted under terms and a similar adjustment will not be made again. Complaints line is absent.

### Variant 4: Decline Further Credit After Prior Credit
- **Position:** Customer received a prior goodwill credit and now demands additional top-ups or refunds for bets placed on other venues.
- **Authority:** One positive-framed decline: the published terms apply to all bets in the sequence and the prior credit addressed its own set.

### Variant 5: Uphold Both (No Chat Advice)
- **Position:** Customer re-bet on their own initiative on a recreated market. Both bets stand as settled on the result. No refund or compensation is due.

## 3. Match Market: 90 Minutes vs To Advance

### Operational Mechanics
- **Rule 12.1:** All football match markets settle on 90 minutes of regular play, including stoppage time, unless explicitly stated otherwise. Extra time and penalty shootouts do not count.
- **The Three Pillars of Defense:**
  1. **Draw Selection:** The market includes a "Draw" selection. An overall knockout winner market would never carry a Draw option.
  2. **Alternative Market:** The separate "To advance" market (Rule 12.5.20) was available for the event and covers extra time and penalties.
  3. **Customer Responsibility:** Rule 2.1 and T&Cs Section 6 place responsibility on the customer to understand market rules prior to placing bets.

## 4. Golf Bet Builder: Dead Heat Void

### Operational Mechanics
- **Rule 9.6.1 (Bet Builder Multiples):** If one or more selections in a Bet Builder are voided or settle as a dead heat, the entire multiple is voided with stake returned, regardless of the results of other legs.
- **The Conflict:** Customer quotes Rule 14.3.2 (dead heat rules apply to Top 5/10/20 markets) and expects a payout reduction factor (e.g. 0.80).
- **The Application:** Acknowledge that Rule 14.3.2 governs single-market dead heats, but Rule 9.6.1 explicitly governs Bet Builders created from the same event and overrides single-market treatment. Standard accumulators across separate events remain available for dead heat reduction factors.

## 5. Horse Racing Reduction Factors

### Operational Mechanics
- **Rule 13.3.2:** Reduction factors reflect the withdrawn horse's percentage chance (odds), not a proportion of matched volume.
- **Rule 13.3.5:** Adjustments apply uniformly to matched bets at or above 2.5%; unmatched orders are cancelled.
- **The 1.00 Floor:** If a reduction factor reduces odds below 1.00, the bet settles at 1.00 with stake returned in full. Note: this floor is operational platform behaviour; attribute to support guidance, never to a published clause number.

## 6. Non-Standard Match Formats (Club Friendlies)

### Operational Mechanics
- **Rule 12.2:** For friendly matches, all bets apply to the full duration of regular time play as officiated by the match officials.
- **Rule Precedence:** Sports-specific Rule 12.2 supersedes General Rule 6.6 ("if the length of the event is modified, all bets are voided"). When a friendly is played as 4x30 quarters, 120 minutes is the officiated full duration and bets stand.

## 7. Unauthorized Access / Compromised Account (Steve Style)

### Operational Mechanics
- **Rules 2.14 and 2.16:** Customers are solely responsible for credentials. Bets placed using valid credentials are treated as made by the account holder and stand as valid.
- **Structure:** Introduce as independent review. Date-anchored chronology (sign-in timestamp, balance reduction via bets). Verbatim quotes of Rules 2.14 and 2.16. Address secondary points (IP investigation, external fund tracing). Direct to Action Fraud. Provide formal IBAS ADR block.

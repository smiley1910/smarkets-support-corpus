## Bank transfer limits and fees

**Withdrawals**
**Fee**Free
**Minimum**£20 / €20 / 250 SEK
**Maximum**N/A

## Processing time

*   Bank transfer withdrawals are processed within 5-10 business days.

## Currencies

*   Bank transfers cannot be transacted in NOK.

## Withdrawal eligibility

*   All withdrawals from Smarkets are subject to our [closed loop policy](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212620285-Withdrawal-limits). Where multiple deposit methods have been used you will be limited to withdrawing up to the deposited amount until net deposits are satisfied across all methods.
*   We can only process a bank transfer withdrawal when all other withdrawal options have been exhausted.
*   If your bank is in the UK, you will need to provide your account number and sort code.
*   If your bank is in the USA, you will need to provide your account number and routing number.
*   If your bank is in the Single Euro Payments Area (SEPA), other than the UK, you will need to provide your IBAN and BIC/SWIFT code.
*   If your bank is in any other country, you will need to provide your account number and sort code or equivalent bank identifier code.
*   You must upload a bank statement showing your name and the above details to verify ownership of the account.
*   Your Smarkets account must be verified. In this case this means you must have provided valid photo ID and your address must be verified either electronically or from a document you have provided Smarkets. You can find the list of accepted documents and find out how to upload documents [here.](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212634845-Documents-required-to-verify-your-account)
*   Your Smarkets currency must match the currency of the bank account that you wish to withdraw to.

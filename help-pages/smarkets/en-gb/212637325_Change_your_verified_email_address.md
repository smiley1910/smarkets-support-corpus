## How can we help you?

[Help Centre](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb)[Safer Gambling](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/categories/360001480391)[Contact Us](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/categories/360001480451)

Your email address is used as your login for Smarkets. Correspondence regarding your account will be sent to your registered email address.

You can change your email address when logged into your Smarkets account.

1. Access your [settings](https://smarkets.com/account/settings)page.

2. Now click on the '[Login](https://smarkets.com/account/settings/login)' tab.

3. Fill in the required information.

Once updated, you will need to verify your new email address. We will send you an email to your updated email address containing a link to verify your email.

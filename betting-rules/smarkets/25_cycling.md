## **25.1 General**

25.1  Markets will be settled on the official result of the relevant governing body where there is a presentation ceremony, at the time of the ceremony, regardless of any subsequent disqualification or amendment.

25.2  Where there is no presentation ceremony, markets will be settled according to the official result of the relevant governing body, regardless of any subsequent disqualification or amendment to the result.

25.3  This will apply except in cases where an amendment is announced within 24 hours of original settlement, in which situations relevant markets may be resettled to correct an error in the initial reporting of the result.

25.4 For outright markets, such as overall race winner or any other classification (points, mountains etc) winner, and stage winner markets bets will bet voided on selections who do not start the race or stage in question.

# Template: Smarkets Final Response — Senior Independent Review (Steve style)

Reusable structure for complaints where position is upheld. Matches the Steve example provided 2026-08-22 (Southampton promotion complaint). Plain text, straight quotes, no em dashes.

## Customer-facing template

Subject: Your complaint - [topic] on [date]

Dear [Customer Name],

Thank you for your patience while we carried out a full review of your complaint. Firstly, I would like to apologise for the delay in providing our final response.

My name is [Name], and I am one of the Senior Customer Support Agents at [Smarkets / SBK]. I have now completed an independent review of your complaint regarding [one-line summary: e.g. unauthorised access on 7 July 2026 and the subsequent loss of £142.73 / your wager on Championship - Promotion involving Southampton].

I understand your position that, [single paragraph summarising customer's view in their terms: e.g. following Southampton's disqualification you believe selection should have been voided / you did not authorise the activity, were victim of cybercrime, never shared password, have no connection to Netherlands, and believe £142.73 should be refunded]. [If second element: I also understand you feel...]

Having carefully reviewed the matter, I can confirm that our position remains unchanged.

[Chronology paragraph — factual, date-anchored, no inference: e.g. On 7 July 2026 at 19:18 you received an email regarding an attempted sign in from the Netherlands. This was followed by notification that a new payment method had been added... Your prior balance of £142.73 was subsequently reduced to £0 through bets placed while logged in with valid credentials.]

For clarity, the applicable [Smarkets Terms and Conditions / Smarkets Betting Rules] state the following:

Rule [X.X]
"[Verbatim quote with straight quotes]"

Rule [Y.Y]
"[Verbatim quote]"

[One-line gloss on discretion if relevant: e.g. Rule 4.5 provides discretion to void or resettle in exceptional circumstances but does not create an obligation to do so. / Rules 2.14 and 2.16 apply whenever valid login credentials are used.]

[Reasoning paragraph tying facts to rules: e.g. The bets that reduced your balance were placed while logged in with your login credentials and were accepted at the time. Under Rule 2.16 they are therefore treated as having been placed by you and stand as valid. / Southampton were disqualified and could no longer achieve promotion, accordingly selection was correctly settled as losing.]

[If prior support position exists: This reflects the position previously provided by our support team, which I have reviewed and confirm is correct under the Terms.]

[Address each secondary customer point in its own paragraph, opening "You asked whether..." or "We also acknowledge your comments that...":

- Investigation ask: "You asked whether we can investigate the IP address..." — state what was checked at outcome level, then "However, the location... does not change the application of Rules..."

- Pursue hacker / trace funds: "You asked why Smarkets does not pursue..." — list platform steps taken, state limit, point to correct external route (Action Fraud / cybercrime).

- Other operators / solicitors thresholds: "We also acknowledge your comments that other operators chose to void / cybercrime and solicitors were unable to progress... While we appreciate your perspective, that does not alter / determine whether [Smarkets/SBK] correctly applied its own published rules..."

- Praise for securing account: "I also note and appreciate your comments that we handled the securing of your account extremely well... I am glad that part was handled well." + 2FA note.

]

In summary, we are satisfied that [account was correctly secured after your report, and] your [wager / balance] has been settled correctly and in accordance with the [Smarkets Terms and Conditions / Betting Rules]. [Rule gloss repeated: Rule 4.5 is discretionary and does not require... / Rules 2.14 and 2.16 apply whenever valid credentials are used and do not provide for a refund...] We are therefore not able to [refund the £X / void the selection].

This email constitutes [Smarkets' / SBK's] final response to your complaint. As such, our position on this matter is final and we will not be revisiting the decision.

If you remain dissatisfied with the outcome, you are entitled to refer your complaint to IBAS for an independent review [with the case number for this being [NUMBER] / If an IBAS case reference is issued we will provide it on request]. ## Notes for Michael

- Source: Steve final response example 2026-08-22 (Championship Promotion — Southampton). Key markers: delay apology, senior independent review intro, "I understand your position that...", "Having carefully reviewed... position remains unchanged", Rules as separate blocks with verbatim quotes, discretion gloss, "We also acknowledge..." for other-operator argument, "In summary, we are satisfied...", final-response lock, IBAS with case number.
- When to use: final position complaints where decision is upheld. Not for holding replies or partial grants.
- Distinguish attempted vs successful login explicitly when customer reports attempted sign in — do not upgrade to successful login.
- Straight quotes only, no em dashes (verified clean).
- Verbatim quotes must be checked against help-pages file before sending.

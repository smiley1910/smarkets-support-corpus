# Payment Name Mismatch — Scenarios & Resolution

## Context

The existing `customer-policy-faq.md` covers closed-loop payments and third-party payments (no third-party deposits/withdrawals, name on payment method must match the account holder). This file covers what happens when there IS a mismatch and how to resolve it.

## Common Scenarios & Resolution

### 1. Name on payment method doesn't match due to typo/minor difference

**Example:** Account says "Mike Smith", card says "Michael Smith"

**What happens:** Withdrawal likely rejected by Smarkets' automated payment validation.
- If the names are clearly the same person (common abbreviation): contact support with ID to verify
- Smarkets may manually approve the withdrawal

### 2. Name change (marriage, divorce, deed poll)

**Example:** Account opened as "Jane Doe", married and now all cards/banks are "Jane Smith"

**What happens:** Name on payment method ≠ account name → stop.
**Resolution:** Contact support@smarkets.com with:
- Photo ID in NEW name (passport, driving licence)
- Name change certificate (marriage certificate, deed poll)
- They update the account name → withdrawals proceed

### 3. Joint bank account

**Example:** Card and bank account are in both spouses' names, but only one name is on the Smarkets account

**What happens:** Grey area. Smarkets T&Cs 5.1 says "identical to that of the account holder." A joint account includes the account holder's name but also someone else's.
**Resolution:** Support ticket required — may be approved or may be rejected depending on the compliance officer. No documented policy.

### 4. Withdrawing to a different method than deposited

**Example:** Deposited via Mastercard, trying to withdraw to bank account.

**What happens:** Method mismatch, not name mismatch, but same practical result — rejected.
**Resolution:** Smarkets generally returns withdrawals to the original funding method. Mastercard withdrawals have been reported as limited to the amount deposited via that card (per forum reports). Withdraw winnings via bank transfer by contacting support (may require additional verification).

### 5. Third-party payment detected

**Example:** Friend deposits on your behalf, or you use a card in a family member's name

**What happens:** Smarkets T&Cs Section 5.1: "If Smarkets identifies that funds have been deposited to your account from a third party, we reserve the right to immediately return the deposit back to the source and void any winnings that have arisen from such a deposit."
**Resolution:** Avoid entirely. If already happened, the deposit and any winnings from it are at risk.

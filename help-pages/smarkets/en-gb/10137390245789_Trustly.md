# Trustly

## About Trustly

Trustly is a fast, simple and secure way to make deposits directly from your bank account. If you have an account with any of the major banks in the UK, you can use Trustly to make deposits and to receive your winnings.

This method is free to use and no app download or sign up is required. There is also no need to add any account or card numbers.

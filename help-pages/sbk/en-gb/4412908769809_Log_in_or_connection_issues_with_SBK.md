If you're experiencing issues logging in to your SBK account or connecting to the live servers, please try the following:

1.   Make sure your internet connection is working.
2.   Log out and then log back into your SBK account.
3.   Ensure you're not using a VPN.
4.   Re-install the app.

If you are still experiencing issues, please [contact customer support.](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360030913931-Contacting-SBK)

**Who can take part?**

*   New customers only. Available to residents of the United Kingdom, Republic of Ireland or Malta.
*   Sign up offers are limited to one per person, family, household address, email address, IP address or shared computer. We reserve the right to withdraw the availability of free bet offers to any customers.

**What do I need to do?**

*   Open a new SBK account.
*   Deposit via Debit Card, Bank Transfer or Trustly and place a minimum £20 / €10 bet at minimum odds of 3.0.
*   **Receive your Free Bet Tokens within 48 hours of your qualifying bet settling.**

**When will I get my bonus?**

*   Please refer to jurisdiction specific terms [here.](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/8882902834461-Welcome-bonus-T-Cs-Bet-10-get-30-in-Free-Bets)

**Where can I see how much promo credit I have remaining?**

*   Tap 'Funds' at the bottom of the SBK app and you will see your Promo balance directly beneath your cash balance at the top.
*   When you place a free bet using the promo credit this amount will update accordingly.

**What else do I need to know?**

*   The offer can only be redeemed by users who make their first deposit on SBK after **16:00 BST on 20th April 2026**and are not existing Smarkets Exchange users.
*   Free bet stakes are not returned on winning bets.
*   Unused bonuses expire after 5 days of being credited to a user account and will be removed after this period.
*   Deposits made with PayPal, a virtual/prepaid card or e-wallets including Skrill, Paysafe and Neteller, will not qualify for the offer. Please note that this includes virtual Monzo cards, first deposits made by this method will not qualify for the welcome offer. Only physical debit card, bank transfer or Trustly deposits are eligible.
*   Customers are only eligible for one sign up promotion.

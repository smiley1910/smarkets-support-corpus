## **Survey period**

The survey is open from **15 December 2025, 17:00 UTC** until **22 December 2025, 17:00 UTC** (the **Survey Period**). Entries submitted after the end of the Survey Period will not be eligible for a reward.

* * *

## **Who can take part?**

You can take part if you:

*   Have a verified SBK account; and

*   **Received the survey invitation directly from Smarkets/SBK via email** (an **Invited Participant**).

**Not eligible:** employees of Smarkets (and anyone professionally connected to this promotion).

* * *

## **What do I need to do?**

To qualify for the reward, you must:

*   Complete the survey during the Survey Period;

*   Enter the **same email address** that:

    1.   received the survey invitation email, and

    2.   is connected to your SBK account; and

*   Answer every mandatory question/section (marked with an asterisk “*”) and successfully submit the survey.

* * *

## **What will I receive?**

This promotion offers a **guaranteed reward** for every eligible, successfully completed survey submission:

*   **UK customers:****£5** in Free Bets

*   **Ireland customers:****€5** in Free Bets

**O****nly Invited Participants** are eligible.

Important notes on Free Bets

*   Free bets can be placed on any Event.
*   If your free bet is settled as a win, the winnings will be added to your withdrawal balance.
*   If a free bet has been used on a selection that has been voided then the free bet stake will be returned.
*   Free bets that have not been used will expire 14 days after they have been credited to your promo balance.
*   Free bets cannot be redeemed for cash at any time.

* * *

## **When will the Free Bets be credited?**

Free Bets will be credited on **22 December 2025 (18:00 UTC)** to the SBK account associated with the **same email address used to complete the survey**.

* * *

## **Important notes**

*   **One reward per eligible person/account.** Multiple submissions may be treated as a single entry and only one reward may be credited.

*   Smarkets/SBK reserves the right not to credit the reward if an account is **suspended, closed, or self-excluded** (whether this occurs before or after survey completion).

*   **Standard SBK Terms and Conditions apply**, including any standard Free Bet rules (use, expiry, settlement, and restrictions).

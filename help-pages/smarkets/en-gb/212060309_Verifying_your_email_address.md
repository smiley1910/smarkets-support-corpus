# Verifying your email address

When you signed up you should have received a welcome email with a verification link, please follow this link to verify your email address.

If you haven't received the verification email kindly, make sure that your email address has been entered correctly by checking [here.](https://smarkets.com/account/settings/login)

If this verification process does not work, please log out of Smarkets on all devices before attempting to use the verification link again.

If the problem persists please email us at [support@smarkets.com](mailto:support@smarkets.com) from your registered Smarkets email address.

If you already have a registered Smarkets account, you can use the same credentials to access the SBK app.

A 2% standard commission fee will be applied on your net winnings on both platforms due to Smarkets being a betting exchange.

To do this please contact our live support through live chat or [support@getsbk.com](mailto:support@getsbk.com) and we can update your account accordingly and grant you access to both platforms.

Note: Users will not be permitted to have two separate accounts with the same credentials on both platforms.

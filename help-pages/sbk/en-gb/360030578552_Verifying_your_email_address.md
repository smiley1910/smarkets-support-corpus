When you signed up you should have received a welcome email with a verification link, please follow this link to verify your email address.

If this verification process does not work, please log out of SBK on all devices before attempting to use the verification link again.

If the problem persists please email us at [support@getsbk.com](mailto:support@getsbk.com) from your registered SBK email address.

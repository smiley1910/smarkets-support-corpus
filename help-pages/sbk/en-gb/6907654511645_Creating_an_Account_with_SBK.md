## Step 1: Logged out Experience (to access the app and see our odds)

If you are a new **SBK** user wanting to access the app, checking what's on offer couldn't be easier.

## ![Image 2: Screenshot_2023-01-04-15-40-48-57_432ba4fcfacced6fddb1acdba6f5032f.jpg](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/article_attachments/8601161544733)

Feel free to have a look around and open any of the events available.

_Note: You'll need to complete Step 2 before you can deposit and bet._

## Step 2: Account Creation (so you can place bets)

Now that there's no doubt in your mind that **SBK** is your new favourite sportsbook, you can complete your account sign-up.

Simply tap the orange banner, like the one shown in the image above, and follow the instructions.

Once you’ve tapped the banner, you'll have to do the following:

*   Insert your email address and enter a password (make sure to remember or note down these details, as you'll need them to sign in to your new account)

*   Enter some information like your name, date of birth, and address (we have to ask for this due to regulation and to enhance your security!)

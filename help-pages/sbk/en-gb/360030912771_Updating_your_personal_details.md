If you would like to change your personal details, you can do so by clicking on the Account button on the bottom right of your screen and then clicking on the Personal Information option as shown below.

![Image 2: IMG_1244.jpg](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/article_attachments/4409825247761/IMG_1244.jpg)

You can update the following personal details on the settings page:

*   Contact number
*   Address

_Note: It’s important to keep these details up-to-date as we may need to contact you regarding your account._

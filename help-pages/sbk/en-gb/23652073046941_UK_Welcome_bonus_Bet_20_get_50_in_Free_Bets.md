## **Full T&Cs**

1.   You must make a first deposit of at least £20/ €20 in a single amount to be eligible for the new customer welcome offer.

2.   A**single** bet of £20 (or greater) must have been **placed and settled** at **minimum odds of 3.0 (2/1).** For example, a £10 each-way bet will not qualify; the total stake must be at least £20. Both Bet Builders and Multiples are eligible for this promotion as long as the stake is £20 or more.

3.   Cashed out bets do not qualify towards this promotion.

4.   Receive a £5 Bet Builder and £5 Multiple on settlement. A £10 Free Bet token and £5 Bet Builder after 24 hours. A further £10 Free Bet token, £5 Bet Builder and 2 x £5 Multiple tokens after 48 hours. Free Bet Tokens require a minimum of 3 legs.

5.   The maximum amount of free bets that can be awarded in this offer is £50.

6.   Free bets can only be placed in denominations of the free bet value attributed to a promotion and require a minimum of 3 legs.

7.   Free bets can not be used on full cover bets.

8.   If your free bet is settled as a win, the winnings will be added to your withdrawal balance: A £10 free bet placed on a selection at odds of 3/1 (4.0) that is settled as a win would result in £30 winnings being added to your withdrawable balance, with the £10 free bet stake not returned.

9.   If a free bet has been used on a selection that has been voided then the free bet stake will be returned.

10.   Free bets that have not been used will expire 5 days after they have been credited to your promo balance.

11.   Free bets cannot be redeemed for cash at any time.

12.   The promotion is only open to new users making their first deposit by card or instant bank transfer. Users making their first deposit by Skrill, Neteller, Revolut, PayPal or a virtual/prepaid card will not qualify for this promotion. Please note that this includes virtual Monzo cards, first deposits made by this method will not qualify for the welcome offer.

13.   The new customer welcome offer is strictly limited to one per individual, family, household address, email address, same payment account number, IP address or shared computer.

14.   The offer can only be redeemed by residents of the UK, who sign up and deposit between 16:00 BST on 23rd June 2026 to 16:00 BST on 23rd July 2026, and are not existing SBK/Smarkets Exchange users. Please note any customer that signed up prior to this is eligible for the offer available at the time of signup.

15.   This promotion cannot be used in conjunction with any other sign-up promotion.

16.   This promotion is only available to users 18 years or older.

17.   SBK reserves the right to change or stop this promotion if required for any legal or regulatory reason.

18.   Any customer who has been sent an email stating that they are not eligible for bonuses will not receive any free bets, even if they meet all other terms of this promotion.

## **18.1 General**

18.1 Tournament point and try bets are applicable to full playing time, including extra-time in matches where an official result is declared.

18.2**Full time result**: This market will be settled on which team has won after regular time has expired or if there is a draw. In markets where draw is not offered as a selection, the market will be settled based on the result after overtime.

18.3 **Total Score**: This market will be settled on the sum of the points scored after regular time has expired.

18.4**Half-time/full-time winners**: This market will be settled on the result at half-time and the result at end of regular time.

18.5**Handicap**: This market will be settled based on the result of the match at the end of regular time with the stated handicap is applied.

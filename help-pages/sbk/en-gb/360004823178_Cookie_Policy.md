## **Cookie Policy**

Cookies are used to perform essential site operations, enhance user experience, tailor advertising and other content to reflect your specific interests.

By using SBK’s website and application you agree to the use of cookies and similar technologies for the purposes we outline in this policy.

## **What is a website cookie?**

Cookies are information in the form of tiny text files that a website stores in a user's browser and can retrieve while a user is browsing the website to improve their online experience and ensure that content and functions are delivered and used more effectively.

## **What type of cookies does SBK track?**

**Tracking**

These cookies collect information about how visitors use and interact with SBK. These cookies do not collect data that identifies a visitor, and are only used to improve SBK’s website performance. If you do not wish to receive third-party analytics cookies, please enable your browser's ‘Do Not Track’ mode.

**Third party**

From time to time, Smarkets may use cookies to deliver advertising and other content to reflect your specific interests (for example those used by Google Analytics or Facebook to deliver targeted ads). Third-party vendors use cookies to serve adverts based on a user's past visits to the Smarkets website. Any data collected will be used in accordance with our own[Privacy Policy](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360030912451-Privacy-Policy). If you do not wish to receive third-party analytics cookies, please enable your browser's ‘Do Not Track’ mode.

_Note: We recommend that you permit the use of cookies. If you choose to disable cookies by changing the settings in your browser then you may find that certain aspects of the website do not work as well as they should, and we do not accept any liability to you for any losses you may incur as a result of this._

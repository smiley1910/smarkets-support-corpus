# 36. Gaelic Games rules

Smarkets [general betting rules](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212183509-1-11-General-Smarkets-betting-rules) (1-11) will apply unless superseded by the [Terms and Conditions](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/213469085-Smarkets-Terms-and-Conditions) or sports specific betting rules.

## **36.1 General**

**‘**Gaelic Games’ applies to both ‘Gaelic Football’ & ‘Hurling’ sports.****

Gaelic Games markets apply to the standard length of 70 minutes including stoppage/injury time unless stated otherwise. Markets for standard 70-minute games do not take extra-time or penalty shootouts into account.

## **36.2****Types of Gaelic Games Markets Offered**

36.2.1 **Full-time result**: This market will be settled on which team has won after regular time has expired or if there is a draw.

36.2.2 **Handicap Betting:**This market will be settled on the final result and taking into consideration the handicap indicated.

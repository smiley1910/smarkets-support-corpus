When you sign up for an SBK account, you may opt in to receive marketing and promotional communication.

You can opt in or out from marketing/promotional communications by doing the following:

*   Click on the Account button on the bottom right
*   Click on the Preferences option as shown below

![Image 2: File.jpg](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/article_attachments/4409825253905/File.jpg)

*   Scroll down to find the Offers and Promotion subheading at the very bottom. Here you can choose to opt in or opt out from receiving these notifications.

![Image 3: File__1_.jpg](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/article_attachments/4409832863761/File__1_.jpg)

_Note: You will still receive account-related emails._

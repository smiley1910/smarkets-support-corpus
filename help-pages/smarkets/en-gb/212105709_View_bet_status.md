Title: View bet status

URL Source: https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212105709-View-bet-status

Markdown Content:
You can quickly view the status of any bet in the ‘Your Bets’ section. By clicking on the "All Active" drop-down, you'll be able to filter a specific bet status for the following;

![Image 1: Screenshot_2022-02-21_at_11.30.10.png](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/article_attachments/4452151038481/Screenshot_2022-02-21_at_11.30.10.png)

Only fully matched bets and the matched portion of partially matched bets will stand. All unmatched bets and the unmatched portion of partially matched bets will be automatically canceled when the market is next halted.

![Image 2: Screenshot_2022-02-21_at_11.31.05.png](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/article_attachments/4452166145681/Screenshot_2022-02-21_at_11.31.05.png)

_Note: When placing multiple bets on the same outcome, bets will be combined in ‘Your Bets’ to display your overall position on each outcome. The total stake and liability, as well as the average odds, will be shown._

# Mailing list

When you sign up to a Smarkets account, unless you opt out, you are automatically opted in to receive marketing and promotional communication. You can opt out from marketing/promotional communication if you wish via the ['General' tab on the settings page](https://smarkets.com/account/settings/general).

_Note: You will still receive account-related emails._

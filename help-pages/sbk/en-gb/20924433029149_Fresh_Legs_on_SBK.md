SBK will now roll your bet over to the substitute who replaces them. Place a bet on a qualifying market and make a profit if the winner is either the player you have bet on or the substitute that replaces them. Fresh legs is only available on markets which display the Fresh Legs icon. Eligible markets are subject to change.

**Example:**You have bet on Phil Foden as the first goalscorer in a match:

1.   Phil Foden scores the first goal of the match. Phil Foden wins ✅
2.   Erling Haaland scores the first goal of the match before any substitutions. Phil Foden lost ❌
3.   While the score is 0-0 Phil Foden is substituted for Jack Grealish. Jack Grealish scores the first goal of the match. Jack Grealish **and**Phil Foden win ✅
4.   While the score is 0-0 Phil Foden is substituted for Jack Grealish. Erling Haaland scores the first goal of the match. Phil Foden lost ❌

For Shots on Target markets we will combine the number of shots on target had by player A who starts the match with player B, who replaces them, when determining the settlement of bets.

**Example:**You have bet on Marcus Rashford over 2.5 shots on target:

1.   Marcus Rashford has 3 shots on target in the match. Marcus Rashford wins ✅
2.   Marcus Rashford has 1 shot on target before being subbed off. His replacement has no shots on target. Marcus Rashford lost ❌
3.   Marcus Rashford has 1 shot on target before being subbed off. His replacement has 2 shots on target. Marcus Rashford wins with 3 shots on target ✅ (1 SoT with his replacements figure 2 SoT)

For the avoidance of doubt, player B would still be settled based on the number of shots on target they have alone after being subbed on.

**How can I identify which matches and markets fresh legs are available on?**

You can identify which matches and markets are available by looking for the Fresh Legs Icon

**Which markets will be available?**

You can expect to see fresh leg markets available for

*   First, Last & Anytime Goalscorer
*   Multi-Scorer (2+ Goals, 3+ Goals)
*   Player Shots on Target (0.5, 1.5, 2.5)
*   Player to be carded or Sent Off
*   Player to score or assist
*   Anytime assists
*   Player Fouls (0.5, 1.5, 2.5)

_Markets are subject to change._

**Is this available for singles, multiples and bet builders?**

Yes, if you add a selection from a fresh legs market the same rules will apply whether the selection is on its own or combined with others.

**Can I use a free bet or bet boost on fresh leg markets?**

Each free bet or bet boost available is different, please refer to the terms of the promotion for confirmation.

**What happens if my match goes to extra time?**

Fresh leg markets will be settled on the regular time results only.

## How can we help you?

[Help Centre](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb)[Safer Gambling](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/categories/360001480391)[Contact Us](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/categories/360001480451)

If you're experiencing issues logging in to your Smarkets account or connecting to the live servers, please try the following:

1.   Make sure your internet connection is working.
2.   Log out and then log back in to your Smarkets account.
3.   Clear Smarkets cookies ([this article explains how to do this](http://www.digitaltrends.com/computing/how-to-delete-cookies-in-chrome-firefox-safari-and-ie/)).
4.   Use a different browser (this will solve a temporary cookie-related issue).
5.   Ensure you're not using a VPN.

If you are still experiencing issues, please contact customer support.

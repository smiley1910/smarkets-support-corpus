Common queries and questions about promo balance and free bets. If you have any other questions please don't hesitate to [contact us.](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360030913931)

*   **Will I get the stake back from a winning free bet?**No, the stake of the free bet is not included. If you place a £10 free bet at odds of 3.0 you will receive just the £20 winnings when the bet settles.
*   **My bet was voided, will I get my free bet back?**Yes, you will get the free bet back, but this can take up to 2 hours to be credited.
*   **My bet is part of a multiple, with one selection voided. What happens to my bet?**If your bet is:
    *   **Bet Builder:** If one selection is voided, the whole bet will be voided.
    *   **Accumulator:** If one selection is voided, the odds will be reduced to account for the voided selection.

*   **Can I split my free bet into smaller amounts?**No, the token can only be used in a singular specified amount.
*   **Can I place cash and free bets on the same selection at the same time?** No, you cannot place a free bet and a cash bet on the same selection in one go. However, you can place a second, separate bet on the same selection if you wish to do so.
*   **What about each-way bets?**An each-way bet is split into two portions. As £2 is currently the minimum free bet stake, you would need to have at least £4 of promo balance to place £2 on the winner market and £2 on the place market.
*   **Are there any events/ odds I can’t use a free bet on?**No, any market (including accumulators) can be bet on and at any odds unless otherwise stipulated in the specific terms of the promotion.
*   **Do the free bets expire?**Promo balance will typically expire 7 days after being credited unless otherwise stipulated in the specific promotion terms.
*   **Why can't I see the promotions section on the account tab?**Please make sure you have the latest version of the app and let us know if you still cannot see this after updating.
*   **Can I cash out free bets?** No, a bet placed using a free bet can't be cashed out.

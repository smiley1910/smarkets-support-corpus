# Top tips to bet safely

Sports betting can be an extremely enjoyable pastime, to ensure you stay in control and gamble safely, why not try some of our top tips:

*   Treat gambling as a source of entertainment, not a source of income.
*   Budget - only spend what you can afford and are willing to lose.
*   [Set limits](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/16453425311773-Setting-deposit-loss-and-stake-limits) and stick to them.
*   Walk away from your losses - never chase.
*   Notice your feelings - avoid gambling when feeling angry and/or upset.
*   Make sure gambling is not your only interest or hobby - keep other activities in the mix.
*   Take lots of breaks, consider a [timeout](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/16453422623261-Take-a-timeout) or [self-exclusion](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/16453545469725-How-to-self-exclude) if you feel it is necessary.
*   Avoid gambling under the influence of alcohol and/or drugs.
*   Seek help if you believe gambling has become a problem.

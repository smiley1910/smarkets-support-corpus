SBK only accepts members of legal gambling age (18). We want betting to be an enjoyable experience and do not want anyone to suffer any hardship, or cause their families or friends to suffer as a result of out of control gambling.

SBK offers a [range of tools](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/en-gb/sections/16456366459933-Safer-Gambling-Toolkit), and information for [support organisations](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360030909971-Support-agencies-for-gambling-addiction-UK), to assist anyone who believes they are developing a gambling problem.

Where we believe a customer is showing signs of problem gambling we may take action, including but not necessarily limited to, suspending accounts, imposing time-outs or limits.

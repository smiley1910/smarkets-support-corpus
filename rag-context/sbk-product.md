## 1. WHAT IS SBK

SBK is Smarkets' **mobile sportsbook** app (iOS/Android). Mission: "Rewriting The Sportsbook" — market-best odds, no old-school bookmaker mentality.

- **Bookmaker model:** the house takes the other side of every bet. No back/lay peer-to-peer matching exposed to the user.
- **App-first** (mobile). No public web interface for placing bets.
- **Shared account system** with Smarkets: same credentials work on both (contact support to enable dual access). Cannot hold two separate accounts with same credentials.
- **2% commission** on net winnings applies on both SBK and Smarkets (because Smarkets is an exchange).
- SBK general betting rules (1–11) are based on Smarkets' rules; apply unless superseded by SBK T&Cs or sport-specific rules.
- **Jurisdictions:** UK, Republic of Ireland, Malta (some promos also Indiana, US).
- **Contact:** support@getsbk.com · Live Chat (logged-in, 09:00–23:00 GMT) · Phone +44 207 617 7413 (09:00–19:00) · Twitter @sbk · Post: 1 Commodity Quay, St. Katharine Docks, London E1W 1AZ.

**Account creation:** In-app only. Browse odds logged-out; tap orange banner to sign up. Requires email, password, name, DOB, address (regulatory/KYC).

## 2. HOW SBK WORKS (Bookmaker, Not Exchange)

### Betting model
Customer bets **against SBK** (the house). SBK sets odds and accepts the stake. No order book, no lay side, no matching against other users (from customer's view). Odds set by SBK using worldwide data sources.

### Dynamic pricing & stake-size impact
Odds can move between adding to slip and placing bet. Two reasons:
1. **Dynamic pricing** — odds move rapidly, especially in-play (e.g., a sending-off).
2. **Stake size** — larger stakes may get slightly reduced odds to manage risk. SBK does **not** turn down bets or close accounts for winning. Better odds offered up to a stake threshold; above it, odds worsen slightly. Example: £20 at 22/1, £100 at 21/1 — but 5 × £20 at 22/1 may each get the better price.

### Stakes & liability
- Min stake: **£/€0.05** · Max per bet: **£/€99,999.99** (larger via multiple bets).
- Liability = stake, deducted instantly on placement, returned if cancelled.

### Net settlement
Multiple bets on same market consolidate into one net settlement (winnings − losses) in transaction history.

### Voided / cancelled bets
- **Cancelled:** market halted before confirmation (event start/end, material event in scouted football, non-runner in racing). Stake returned.
- **Voided:** outcome cannot be determined (postponement/cancellation, non-runner in non-ante-post market, bets after outcome known).
- Markets voided for postponement only if event not concluded within **36 hours** of original start.
- SBK can void/resettle if new info emerges (disqualification, cheating) or if settled in error.

### Reduction factors (horse racing)
When a horse is withdrawn, remaining odds are adjusted (fewer runners = higher probability each). Similar to Tattersalls Rule 4.
- **'To win':** New odds = Old odds × ((100 − RF) / 100)
- **'To place':** New winnings = Old winnings × ((100 − RF) / 100)

## 3. BETTING ON SBK

### Bet types
- **Singles, Doubles, Trebles, Accumulators** — selections from different events
- **Same-game multiples (Bet Builders)** — multiple selections from the SAME event
- **Full cover bets** — Patent, Trixie, Lucky 15, Yankee, Lucky 31, Super Yankee (see §5)
- **Each-way** and **Outright** bets on applicable markets

### In-play betting
- Delay: **4 seconds** (horse racing, darts) or **8 seconds** (all other events).
- Markets halted at start/end and on material events (goals, red cards, penalties).
- Customers should manage in-play bets at all times — exact halting not guaranteed.

## 4. MULTIPLES & ACCUMULATORS

Two types with different rules:

**Standard multiple** (selections from DIFFERENT events):
- Voided selection → odds reduced, bet continues. Reduction factors reduce acca odds accordingly.
- Cash out allowed while events are in-play. Available on any sport. Max 10 selections. No outright selections.

**Same-game multiple / Bet Builder** (selections from SAME event):
- One+ voided/dead-heated selection → **whole bet voided**, stake returned (even if some legs already lost). Racing bet builders voided if any reduction factor applied.
- Cash out **unavailable** once event in-play. Available on **football, American football, basketball, tennis** only. Max 10 selections. No outright selections.
- Eligible markets found under "Bet Builder" category tab in an event.

**How to place:** Add selections to slip → toggle on/off → enter stake → place. All selections must win. Multiple price formed from original odds but not an exact accumulation.

## 6. CASH OUT

Lock in profit or cut losses, pre-game and in-play. Amount reflects bet performance.

- **Mechanism:** SBK places an immediate counter bet against your original. Amount credited instantly on success.
- **Access:** My Bets → 'Upcoming' tab → tap cash out → confirm.
- **NOT guaranteed:** fails if market suspended or odds move before processing. On failure: reason shown + new offer may appear.
- **In-play delays:** 4s (racing/darts) / 8s (other). Accumulators with live selections may have extended delays.
- **Accumulator cash out:** button doesn't show price immediately — tap to view offer. **Do not repeatedly tap** (causes lag, may place without confirmation or double-cash-out).
- **Outright cash out:** available under 'Outright' tab in My Bets.
- **When unavailable:** market suspended, or event not covered/stopped being covered in-play.
- **Voiding after cash out:** if bet ultimately voided, no winnings/losses; only stake returned. Pre-void cash-out winnings removed (or losses returned) at settlement.

## 8. SBK-SPECIFIC FEATURES

### SBK Best Price
Compares SBK odds vs **8 competitors**: SkyBet, Ladbrokes, Unibet, Coral, Betway, BetVictor, Paddy Power, William Hill. **Green chevrons** appear when SBK has best/joint-best odds (absent if worse or data stale). Post-bet banner shows best/joint-best/better-elsewhere; click for provider breakdown. Data refreshed every **30 seconds**; prices >15 min old discarded. **Excludes** promos/boosts. Does NOT support: in-play, bet builders, full cover, each-way bets.

### Fresh Legs
**Substitution protection** for football player bets. If your player is subbed off, bet rolls to their replacement. Markets: Goalscorer (First/Last/Anytime), Multi-Scorer, Player Shots on Target, Player to be carded/sent off, Player to score or assist, Anytime assists, Player Fouls. Goalscorer: bet wins if EITHER original or replacement scores (after sub, score unchanged). Shots on Target: combines shots from starter + replacement. Settled on regular time only. Works for singles, multiples, bet builders. Eligible competitions: major UK/European football leagues + MLS + World Club Cup. Identified by Fresh Legs icon.

### Fresh Legs — Customer Choice (toggle)
Toggle **ON/OFF** on eligible football events. ON = insurance, slightly lower odds (default). OFF = no insurance, industry-best prices. Toggle under Player Markets tabs. Preferences: Enabled for all / Choose every time / Disabled for all (Settings → SBK Preferences → Fresh Legs).

### Live Activity (iOS)
Follow football matches from iPhone **Lock Screen** — no app open needed. Shows pre-match (teams, kits, kick-off, lineups), in-play (time, score, stats), post-match (final score). Activate: tap 📌 Pin icon on event page or My Bets (app v2.3.1+). Requires **iOS 16.1+**; enable in Settings → SBK → Live Activities. **Football only.** Stop: tap 'x'/End on card or unpin in-app.

### Advanced Payout
Full cover bets settle legs **as they complete** — not all at once. Running return shown in My Bets.

## 10. TECHNICAL ISSUES

### Login issues
Causes: invalid email/password, suspended account. Invalid password → forgotten password procedure (too many attempts → blocked → contact support). Suspended → contact support (most common cause: verification documents awaited).

### Log in / connection troubleshooting
1. Check internet. 2. Log out and back in. 3. Disable VPN. 4. Re-install app. 5. Contact support.

### Connectivity issues
Disconnected notice → click to reconnect. Persistent: unstable internet/3G-4G, VPN/proxy, other software blocking.

### App unavailability
Planned/unplanned downtime. Updates via app messages, @SmarketsTech Twitter, status.smarkets.com. Support available during downtime (email, phone, live chat by severity).

### Odds changing on placement
Normal — dynamic pricing (in-play moves) + stake-size impact. NOT an error. SBK never turns down bets or closes accounts for winning.

### Account restrictions (verification)
- **Deposit block** — cannot deposit until verified.
- **Bet block** — cannot place/alter bets until verified.
- **Temporary suspension** — cannot log in until verified.

### Closed loop system (payments)
Withdrawals must use same method as initial deposit (AML compliance). **Net Deposits = Deposits − Withdrawals** (per method). Must balance positive net deposits on each method before withdrawing elsewhere. If card no longer in use: deposit with new card then remove old, or contact support with verification docs (PDF bank statements, card photo).

## 11. DIFFERENCES FROM SMARKETS EXCHANGE

| Aspect | SBK (Sportsbook) | Smarkets (Exchange) |
|--------|-------------------|---------------------|
| **Model** | Bookmaker — house takes other side | Peer-to-peer — users back/lay vs each other |
| **Odds** | Set by SBK (dynamic, stake-size sensitive) | User-driven order book (bid/ask) |
| **Commission** | 2% on net winnings | 2% on net winnings |
| **Lay betting** | Not available to users | Users can lay outcomes |
| **Cash out** | Counter bet by SBK; instant | Manual trade-out (opposite bet) |
| **Multiples/accas** | Native (singles to accas, bet builders, full cover) | Not native consumer feature |
| **Bet Feed** | Social timeline (share/copy/counter) | Not available |
| **Best Price** | Odds comparison vs 8 competitors | Not available (user-driven prices) |
| **Fresh Legs** | Substitution protection | Not available |
| **Live Activity** | iOS lock-screen tracking | Not available |
| **Advanced Payout** | Full cover legs settle as completed | Not applicable |
| **Promotions** | Welcome bonus, Acca Boost, SB10K | Not applicable |
| **Account** | Same credentials as Smarkets (dual via support) | Same credentials as SBK |
| **Platform** | Mobile app only | Web, app, and API |
| **Winning accounts** | Never closed for winning (stake-size adjustment instead) | Never closed for winning |
| **In-play delays** | 4s (racing/darts) / 8s (other) | Same (shared infrastructure) |
| **Keep bets in-play** | Not user-facing | Users keep unmatched bets through halts |
| **Betting rules** | Based on Smarkets rules (1–11), superseded by SBK T&Cs | Original general rules (1–11) |
| **Payments** | Closed loop (deposit method = withdrawal method) | Same closed loop applies |

**Shared infrastructure note:** SBK's rules reference Smarkets infrastructure (Instant Match, price-time priority, Hanson Applied Sciences as liquidity provider). The 2% commission exists on both because Smarkets is an exchange. However, **from the customer's perspective, SBK functions entirely as a traditional sportsbook** — bets are placed against the house.

## 12. KEY ESCALATION TRIGGERS

- **Account suspended/blocked** → check verification documents; direct to support@getsbk.com.
- **Cannot withdraw** → check closed loop (net deposits per method); may need to balance a method first.
- **Cash out failed** → normal if market suspended/odds moved; new offer may appear.
- **Bonus not received** → check qualifying bet settled within 48h; check deposit method (no e-wallets/virtual cards).
- **Odds changed on placement** → explain dynamic pricing + stake-size impact; not an error.
- **Same-game multiple voided entirely** → correct behaviour; one voided leg voids the whole bet builder.
- **App unavailable** → check status.smarkets.com / @SmarketsTech; support available during downtime.

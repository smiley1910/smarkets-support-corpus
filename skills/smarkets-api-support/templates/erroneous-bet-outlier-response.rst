.. Variables to replace:
..   {selection_name}  — e.g. "Humza Bey"
..   {bet_id}          — e.g. "203580437510389770"
..   {fair_odds}       — determined fair market price in decimal odds, e.g. "3.4"
..   {bet_odds}        — odds the bet was matched at, e.g. "10.0"
..   {fair_pct}        — fair odds as percentage price, e.g. "29.4"
..   {threshold_pct}   — fair_pct minus 20, e.g. "9.4"
..   {threshold_odds}  — 100 / threshold_pct, e.g. "10.63"
..

Thank you for your chat last night.

I have had the bet for the lay of ``{selection_name}`` (bet ID ``{bet_id}``) reviewed by our trading team.

As your query was raised outside the 60-minute reporting window, the bet was assessed under our outlier transactions policy. The true odds of ``{selection_name}`` were ``{fair_odds}``. The lay placed at ``{bet_odds}`` was deemed not to be an outlier bet, as per our rules on erroneous betting. For a bet to be classified as an outlier, it must exceed +-20% of the fair market price. ``{bet_odds}`` was within this threshold -- our policy calculates price differences in percentage price terms, so fair value of ``{fair_odds}`` equates to ``{fair_pct}%``, and 20 percentage points below gives ``{threshold_pct}%`` (decimal odds of ``{threshold_odds}``). As this is the case, the bet would stand.

Please see below for our rules on outlier transactions:

  In the case of outlier transactions Smarkets may, at its sole and absolute discretion and on a case by case basis, carry out investigations into trades beyond the 60 minute reporting window or after events have been settled.

  Outlier transactions are defined as bets matched at a price that exceeds +-20% of the determined fair market price at the time the bet was matched.

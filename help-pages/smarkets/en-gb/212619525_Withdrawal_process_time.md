Title: Withdrawal process time

URL Source: https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212619525-Withdrawal-process-time

Markdown Content:
Withdrawal process times vary depending on your chosen deposit method. The table below explains the withdrawal process time for each payment option:

**Payment option****Time**
[Open Banking](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360006230237-Open-Banking)12 hours
[Visa debit card](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212009749-Visa-Debit)1-6 business days
[Solo / Maestro card](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212607305-Maestro)1-6 business days
[Visa credit card](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212607345-Visa-Credit-Card)1-6 business days
[MasterCard / Eurocard](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212608105-Visa-Mastercard-Eurocard)1-6 business days
[Neteller](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212008689-NETELLER)1-2 business days
[Skrill](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212008509-Skrill)1-2 business days
[Bank Transfer](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212009689-Bank-Transfer)5-10 business days
[Trustly](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360003024392)1-2 business days
[PayPal](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360019121771-PayPal)1-2 business days

_Note: If clients_ withdraw more than 4 times in a 7 day period from an e-wallet payment method, the fourth and further withdrawals will take 24hr to process.

_Note: If your withdrawal has not reached your financial account in the time frame stated above please ensure the withdrawal was complete and the funds debited from your Smarkets account. If there is still an issue please contact [support@smarkets.com](mailto:support@smarkets.com)._

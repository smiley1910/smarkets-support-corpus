# Minimum and maximum stakes

![Image 2](https://help.smarkets.com/system/photos/114152635491/Twitter-avatar.jpg)

 Smarkets 
*   4 years ago

[Follow](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/4412908289809-Minimum-and-maximum-stakes/subscription.html "Opens a sign-in dialogue")

The minimum stake is £/€ 0.05 and the maximum stake per bet is £/€ 99,999.99. You can stake larger amounts by placing multiple separate bets.

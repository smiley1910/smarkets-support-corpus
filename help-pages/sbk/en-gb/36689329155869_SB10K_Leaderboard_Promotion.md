### **Full T&Cs**

1.   **Only users who opt-in to this promotion will have their overall profit/loss counted in the leaderboard.**
2.   Only profit/loss from wagers placed and settled after opting in will be eligible for the promotion and displayed on the leaderboard
3.   Once opted-in, customers will be able to opt-out of this promotion by contacting SBK's customer service team via email - support@getsbk.com
4.   You must have made at least one deposit since registering your SBK account to participate.
5.   The promotional period runs from **00:01 BST on Tuesday 9th June, 2026 to 02:00 BST on Monday 20th July, 2026.**
6.   **Leaderboard rankings are determined by overall profit / loss from qualifying bets placed on the World Cup 2026.** Only bets placed with **minimum****odds of 1.5** will qualify towards the leaderboard.
7.   Only bets placed and settled on the World Cup 2026 during the promotional period will count towards the promotion.
8.   Outright Bets placed at any time will not count towards this promotion.
9.   **A minimum of £30 must be wagered each week in order to qualify for this promotion (total £120).**
10.   Only customers using a later version of the SBK app than 2.6.3 and are opted into the promotion will be able to view the leaderboard in full.
11.   The top 40 users on the leaderboard at the end of the promotional period will win a share of £10,000, as follows:

    *   1st place: £5,000 cash prize
    *   2nd place: £1,500 cash prize
    *   3rd place: £750 cash prize
    *   4th to 10th: £250 cash prize each
    *   11th to 20th: £50 in Free Bet tokens each, free bets will be broken into denominations of £10.
    *   21st to 40th: £25 in Free Bet tokens each, free bets will be broken into denominations of £5.

12.   Winners will be determined based on the final leaderboard standings at **11:00 BST 20th July, 2026**. Contributing bets must settle prior to 02:00 BST Monday, 20th July, 2026.
13.   In the event that there are more winners than expected, the winnings will be split proportionately.
14.   Cash Prizes and Free Bet Prizes will be credited to the winners account within 72 hours.
15.   Free Bet prizes may be subject to use restrictions and cannot be withdrawn as cash.
16.   Free bets can only be placed in denominations of the free bet value attributed to this promotion, refer to term 11 for further details.
17.   This promotion is limited to one entry per individual, family, household address, email address, payment method, and shared device.
18.   **Any attempts to manipulate the promotion or leaderboard may result in disqualification and forfeiture of prizes.**
19.   Users with accounts that are suspended, self-excluded, or otherwise restricted at any point during the promotional period will not be eligible to participate in this promotion or receive any prizes.
20.   SBK reserves the right to amend or withdraw this promotion at any time prior to placing a qualifying bet. The promotion may be suspended or terminated in cases of fraud, technical issues or regulatory requirements.
21.   Disqualified users will be notified via email and may request a review of their disqualification.
22.   This promotion is only available to primary customers of SBK only, users with access to Smarkets are not able to participate in this promotion. If you would like to amend your account access to become eligible, please contact [support@getsbk.com](mailto:support@getsbk.com).
23.   Offer available to customers in the United Kingdom, Ireland, Malta and Indiana only.
24.   Smarkets employees are not eligible to participate in this promotion.
25.   SBK’s standard terms and conditions and sports specific betting rules apply.

Please gamble responsibly. 18+. BeGambleAware.org.

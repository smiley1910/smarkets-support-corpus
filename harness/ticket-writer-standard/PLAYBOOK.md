# Standard Ticket Writer — Playbook & Canonical Scenarios

## 1. SBK Promo Ineligible for Exchange Users

### Context
An existing Smarkets exchange user attempts to claim an SBK welcome offer or promotion (e.g. SB10K, Bet £20 Get £50).

### Position
Position lands immediately in sentence 1. Smarkets exchange and SBK are separate products with separate balances and terms. SBK promotions are restricted to SBK accounts.

### Canonical Template (~140 words, council verified)
```
Hi [Customer Name],

This promotion is not available to you, as SBK promotions are for SBK sportsbook customers and your account is on the Smarkets exchange.

SBK is our sportsbook, where we set the odds directly. The exchange is a separate product with its own balance, and promotional terms do not extend between them. Under the promotion conditions, users with existing access to the Smarkets exchange are not eligible to participate.

On the exchange you bet against other users rather than against a bookmaker, so the odds are generally better. You can back or lay, trade out before an event finishes, and commission is only charged on net winnings per market, at 2% on most accounts, with nothing taken on losing bets.

If you have any questions about betting on the exchange, just reply to this email.

Kind regards,
[Agent Name] Smarkets Support
```

## 2. Closed-Loop Withdrawal Enquiries (T&Cs 5.11)

### Context
A customer requests a withdrawal to a card or bank account that differs from their deposit method, or asks why a withdrawal was routed to an old card.

### Policy Rules
- Smarkets and SBK operate a closed-loop system for AML compliance.
- Funds must be returned to the same payment method used to deposit wherever possible (T&Cs 5.11).
- Net Deposits = Total Deposits minus Total Withdrawals per method.
- Positive net deposits on any method must be balanced to zero before profits can be withdrawn to another method.
- If a card is expired or cancelled, customer must provide an official document (bank statement or letter from issuing bank confirming card is closed/invalidated) before the method can be removed.

### Phrasing Rules
- Always include the mandatory qualifier: "wherever possible, at our discretion" (do not state closed loop as an absolute unconditional guarantee).
- State the net deposit calculation plainly: "Card ending 1234 has net deposits of £50. This must be balanced before funds can be withdrawn to another method."

## 3. Source of Funds & Source of Wealth (SoF/SoW)

### Context
Customer asks why their account is restricted, why documents are requested, or how long the review will take.

### Policy Rules
- **Clause 2.17:** Routine identity and residency verification. Electronic checks complete in minutes; manual document review is aimed within 24 hours.
- **Clause 2.18:** Enhanced Due Diligence / Source of Funds / Source of Wealth checks required under gambling regulatory authorities and anti-money laundering legislation.
- **No SLA for SoF/SoW:** Help centre documentation states documents are reviewed in a "reasonable timeframe". **Never** cite the 24-hour SLA for SoF/SoW reviews (24 hours is for Clause 2.17 ID checks only).
- **Triggers:** Never invent or state deposit thresholds or trigger criteria ("deposit size triggered review" is an inference). The published policy states: "Not all customers will be required to go through this process."
- **Employer Contact:** Smarkets/SBK will **never** contact an employer. Acceptable evidence includes bank statements, payslips, P60, or tax returns.
- **Restrictions:** Escalating restrictions apply: deposit block -> betting block -> temporary suspension until documentation is reviewed.

## 4. Bet Cancellation & Clearly Erroneous Bets

### Context
Customer placed a bet by mistake (wrong stake, wrong selection) and wants it cancelled or refunded.

### SBK Rules
- SBK bets stand once placed. The bet confirmation screen (or Face ID / biometric confirmation) serves as explicit user confirmation of details before submission.
- **Clearly Erroneous Bets Policy:** Relief is available only where the odds offered deviated by ±15% or more from the fair market price.
- **Timing:** Written request must be emailed to support@getsbk.com within 60 minutes of placement, and in the absence of exceptional circumstances, must be received before the event has started.

### Smarkets Exchange Rules
- Unmatched or partially unmatched bets can be cancelled directly by the customer via the app or website at any time before matching.
- Once a bet is matched on the exchange, it cannot be cancelled by support. The customer must use the trade out functionality on the market.

## 3. Commission

3.1 The default rate of commission on Smarkets is 2% of net winnings in each market.

3.2 Users on the default rate of commission will be limited to a total of 1500 placed bets or £1,000,000 in placed amount staked per calendar month, whichever is reached first.

3.3 Users can opt in to a Pro Tier of commission of 1% on winnings or losses per bet. Users on this tier will not be limited on number of orders or staked amount. The minimum opt-in period for this tier is three calendar months plus the time between opt-in and the end of that month.

3.4 Smarkets may also apply the Pro Tier rate of commission to individual accounts without those accounts meeting the above thresholds (of clause 3.2), most commonly if the account in question is regularly close to meeting the thresholds (of clause 3.2) or when the commission paid under the default structure is not aligned with the account’s trading activity. In such cases, we will contact the account owner via email and notify them prior to the Pro Tier taking effect. If the user chooses to continue trading following this notification, any bets that settle after Pro Tier takes effect will be settled with the Pro Tier commission structure.

3.5 The Select Tier commission rate is a charge which is applied to a very small number of the most profitable users. Users who have made a net profit of over £25,000, or currency equivalent, with Smarkets over the previous 12 calendar months may be switched to the Select Tier commission rate of 3% on winnings or losses per bet. This commission rate will be charged instead of the default rate of commission. Users on the Select Tier commission rate will not be limited on number of bets or staked amount.

3.6 Users whose accounts are eligible to be charged the Select Tier commission rate will be notified of this by email 7 days in advance of their account moving to the Select Tier commission rate.

3.7 Users on the Select Tier commission rate whose profit over the previous 12 calendar months falls below £25,000 can request that their commission be moved back to their previous rate at Smarkets’ discretion by contacting Smarkets’ customer support team.

3.8 All commissions will be calculated and charged at time of market settlement. Bets will be settled based on the user's commission at time of market settlement and not at time of placement.

3.9 Users can request to opt in and out of the Pro Tier by contacting our customer support team by live chat, email, or phone. Smarkets’ decision on any move to or from the Pro Tier will be final.

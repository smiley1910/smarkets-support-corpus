# Updating your personal details

You can update the following personal details on the [settings](https://smarkets.com/account/settings/personal) page:

*   Contact telephone
*   Address

It’s important to keep these details up-to-date as we may need to contact you regarding your account.

Should you need to update your name, or should your date of birth be incorrect, please [contact our support team](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/210638729-Contacting-Smarkets) for assistance.

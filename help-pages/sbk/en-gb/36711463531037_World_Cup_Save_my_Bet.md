Save My Bet is a feature that allows our customers to protect their Bet Builder against a single losing selection.

## **How it works**

Customers can select between **3 and 7 eligible player prop selections within the Ballers feature** from the same event and choose one of two betting options:

**Save My Bet (Default)**

*   Protection against one losing selection.
*   Additional combinations are automatically generated.
*   Total stake increases based on the number of combinations created.

**Example**

A customer selects 5 player prop selections and chooses **Save My Bet**.

The user will only see one bet being placed on their end, but from the back end, we will see that the system creates:

*   1 x 5-fold Bet Builder
*   5 x 4-fold combinations

This results in a total of **6 bets** being placed.

If all 5 selections win:

*   All combinations win and are paid accordingly.

If 4 of the 5 selections win:

*   The 5-fold loses.
*   One of the 4-fold combinations wins.
*   The customer receives the payout from the winning 4-fold.

If 2 or more selections lose:

*   All combinations lose.
*   No payout is awarded.

**Key Points**

*   Available on eligible only on the World Cup events.
*   Requires between 3 and 7 selections.
*   All selections must be from the same event.
*   Save My Bet allows one losing selection while still providing a chance of a return.
*   Additional combinations are generated automatically when Save My Bet is selected.

**Important Stake Information**

When Save My Bet is selected, the entered stake applies to **each generated combination**, not the overall bet.

**Example**

A customer selects 5 player props and enters a £10 stake using Save My Bet.

The system generates:

*   1 x 5-fold
*   5 x 4-folds

Total bets placed: 6

Stake per bet: £10

Total stake charged: £60

**Why is the total stake higher?**

Save My Bet creates multiple individual bets to protect one losing selection.

Because each combination is placed as a separate bet, the total stake is multiplied by the number of generated combinations.

**Want to keep your total stake at a specific amount?**

Remember that your stake is applied to **each generated combination**, not to the overall Save My Bet bet.

If you have a target total stake in mind, divide it by the number of combinations generated.

Using the example above 👆

If you want your **total stake to be £12**, you would need to enter a stake of **£2 per combination**:

£2 × 6 combinations = **£12 total stake**

Always check the total stake shown on the bet slip before placing your bet.

## **How to place a “Save My Bet” bet**

## **Step 1: Select eligible markets**

Click on the “Save My Bet” banner. This will redirect you to the World Cup Ballers page and must select between 3 and 7 eligible player prop selections from the same event.

## Step 2: Add selections to the bet slip + Review stake and potential returns

Once the required number of selections has been added, the customer can view how the bet/s would be paid out by clicking on “Learn more”

## Step 3: Place the bet

Once confirmed, the customer places the Ballers bet.

The bet receipt will show:

*   Ballers type selected. (Save My Bet)

*   Number of combinations generated.

*   Total stake.

*   Potential returns.

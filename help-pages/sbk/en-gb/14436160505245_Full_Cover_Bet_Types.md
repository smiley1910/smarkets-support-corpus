**Patent**

A Patent consists of 7 bets involving 3 selections in 3 different events.

*   The bet includes:

    *   3 single bets
    *   3 double bets
    *   1 treble bet

Just one successful selection guarantees a return.

**Trixie**

A Trixie consists of 4 bets involving 3 selections in 3 different events.

*   The bet includes:

    *   3 doubles bets
    *   1 treble bet

A minimum of 2 successful selections will guarantee a return.

**Lucky 15**

A Lucky 15 consists of 15 bets involving 4 selections in 4 different events.

*   The bet includes:

    *   4 single bets
    *   6 double bets
    *   4 treble bets
    *   1 fourfold bet

Just one successful selection guarantees a return.

**Yankee**

A Yankee consists of 11 bets involving 4 selections in 4 different events.

*   The bet includes:
    *   6 double bets
    *   4 treble bets
    *   1 fourfold bet

A minimum of 2 successful selections will guarantee a return.

**Lucky 31**

A Lucky 31 consists of 31 bets involving 5 selections in 5 different events.

*   The bet includes:

    *   5 single bets
    *   10 double bets
    *   10 treble bets
    *   5 fourfold bets
    *   1 fivefold bet

Just one successful selection guarantees a return.

**Super Yankee**

A Super Yankee consists of 26 bets involving 5 selections in 5 different events.

*   The bet includes:
    *   10 double bets
    *   10 treble bets
    *   5 fourfold bets
    *   1 fivefold bet

A minimum of 2 successful selections will guarantee a return.

## **Full T&C’s**

1. In order to be eligible for this promotion you will need to first opt in in the ‘Promotions’ section in your SBK account.

2. You must then place $100 worth of bets at odds of +100 or greater on any market between 08:00 EDT Wednesday - 23:59 EDT Tuesday, each week. You will then be eligible to receive one $10 Free Bet token to use on Parlays and one $10 Free Same Game Parlay token. Free bets will be credited to your promotional balance once $100 worth of bets have been settled.

3. Bets placed prior to opting in do not count towards this promotion.

4. Your 1 x $10 Free Bet token for Parlays and 1 x $10 Free SGP Token will be credited to your promotional balance upon settlement of the qualifying bet. Parlay and SGP Free Bets will have a minimum of 3 legs.

5. Free Bets are not eligible for use on Full Cover bets.

6. Bets must be placed and settled before 23:59 EDT Tuesday, each week.

7. Any bets placed must have minimum odds of +100

8. Parlays will contribute towards this promotion as long as the cumulative odds are greater than +100.

9. Each way bets will act as two separate bets when contributing towards this promotion. Only bets that are equal to or greater than +100 will contribute towards this promotion.

10. Bets that are cashed out, voided or placed using free bets do not count towards this promotion.

11. Bets placed to meet the requirements of other promotional offers do not count towards this promotion.

12. Free bets do not count towards this promotion.

13. This offer is available to existing SBK customers in Indiana.

14. This offer is strictly limited to one per individual, family, household address, email address, same payment account number, and shared computer.

15. SBK reserves the right in its discretion to exclude, withdraw and/or limit the availability of the offer to certain customers.

16. Free Bets will appear in your promotional balance, have no monetary value and are non-withdrawable.

17. Free Bets can be placed on all sports on any market.

18. Free Bets that have not been used will expire 7 days after they have been credited to your promotional balance.

19. SBK standard terms for promotions apply.

20. Please gamble responsibly, 21+. ncpgambling.org.

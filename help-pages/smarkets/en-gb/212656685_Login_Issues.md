Title: Login Issues

URL Source: https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212656685-Login-Issues

Markdown Content:
If you’re experiencing problems logging in to your Smarkets account, it could be due to one of the following reasons:

*   Invalid email address
*   Invalid password
*   Suspended account

In the case of entering an invalid password, you are able to reset your password by clicking the [Forgot Password](https://smarkets.com/members/forgot-password) link and following the [forgotten password procedure](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212104449-Forgotten-password-procedure). If you log in with the incorrect details too many times your account may be blocked and you will need to contact [support@smarkets.com](mailto:support@smarkets.com).

If your account has been suspended, please contact[support@smarkets.com](mailto:support@smarkets.com)and our customer support team will be able to assist you.

_Note: The most common reason for your account to be suspended is that we are awaiting your verification documents, which will have been requested via email._

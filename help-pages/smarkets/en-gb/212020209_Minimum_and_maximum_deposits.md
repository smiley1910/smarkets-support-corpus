# Minimum and maximum deposits

The minimum and maximum deposit amounts vary depending on your chosen deposit method. The table below explains the amounts for each payment option. To find out more about each payment method, click on the link:

**Payment Option****Limits (£/€)**
[Instant Bank Transfer](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360006230237)£/€10 minimum
[Visa debit card](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212009749-Visa-Debit)£/€10 minimum
[Solo / Maestro card](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212607305-Maestro)£/€10 minimum
[MasterCard / Eurocard](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212608105-Visa-Mastercard-Eurocard)£/€10 minimum
[Neteller](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212008689-NETELLER)£/€10 min / £/€5k daily max
[Skrill](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212008509-Skrill)£/€10 min / £/€5k daily max
[Bank Transfer](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212009689-Bank-Transfer)£/€10 minimum
[Trustly](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360003024392)£/€10 minimum
[PayPal](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360019121771-PayPal)£/€20 min / £/€200 daily max

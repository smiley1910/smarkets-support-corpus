Smarkets [general betting rules](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212183509-1-11-General-Smarkets-betting-rules) (1-11) will apply unless superseded by the [Terms and Conditions](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/213469085-Smarkets-Terms-and-Conditions) or sports specific betting rules.

## **27.1 General**

27.1  Markets will be settled on the official result of the relevant governing body where there is a presentation ceremony, at the time of the ceremony, regardless of any subsequent disqualification or amendment.

27.2  Where there is no presentation ceremony, markets will be settled according to the official result of the relevant governing body, regardless of any subsequent disqualification or amendment to the result.

27.3  This will apply except in cases where an amendment is announced within 24 hours of original settlement, in which situations relevant markets may be resettled to correct an error in the initial reporting of the result.

# Matched betting safely

## **What is matched betting?**

Matched betting is a betting strategy that allows you to benefit from bookmakers’ free bet bonuses, offers and incentives by using Smarkets betting exchange to make a low-risk profit.

For an in-depth explanation of matched betting, visit our [handy guide](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/115003678149-What-is-matched-betting-)!

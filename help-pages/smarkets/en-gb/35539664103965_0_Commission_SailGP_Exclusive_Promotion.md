Title: 0% Commission - SailGP Exclusive Promotion

URL Source: https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/35539664103965-0-Commission-SailGP-Exclusive-Promotion

Markdown Content:
1. This offer will run from 16:30 GMT on Tuesday, February 24th, 2026 onwards and is available to Smarkets bettors who sign up through SailGP and enter the code “SAILGP” when signing up.

2. This offer cannot be used in conjunction with Skrill, Neteller, Trustly or Paypal deposits.

3. To be eligible for the offer, users must complete the following steps in this order:

i) When signing up for their new Smarkets account they must enter code “SAILGP” in the Promo Code field when signing up.

ii) The user must not have received notice from Smarkets that their account is ineligible for the promotion.

4. 0% commission will be activated within 15 minutes of their account being created.

5. The user will be entitled to 0% commission for 365 days after sign up.

6. Smarkets reserves the right to revoke eligibility for the promotion for individual accounts. This will be done by way of an email notification stating that no further extension can be made to the eligibility period. Any bets which settle after the expiry of the eligibility period will settle at standard commission rates.

7. While a customer is eligible, 0% commission will be applied to all bets that settle during the promotional period. Skrill, Neteller, Trustly and Paypal will be made unavailable as deposit methods for the duration of the promotional period.

8. Customers who sign up to Smarkets and don’t use the code “SAILGP” when signing up will be ineligible for this offer.

9. This offer is only available to customers in countries where you are able to operate a Smarkets account, excluding Sweden.

10. [Smarkets T&Cs](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/213469085-Smarkets-Terms-and-Conditions) apply.

Please contact customer support either via live chat or email: support@smarkets.com, should you have any issues integrating your account after following the steps stipulated above in that exact order.

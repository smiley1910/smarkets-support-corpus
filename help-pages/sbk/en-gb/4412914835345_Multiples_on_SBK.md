#### SBK allows you to combine multiple selections from our sports events into a single bet.

## How to place a multiple bet on SBK

Simply add the selections to your bet slip. From your bet slip, you can choose to add or remove selections from the multiple by using the toggle on each selection.

![Image 2: Untitled.png](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/article_attachments/4412914559633/Untitled.png)

You will then need to simply enter your stake in the highlighted box shown above and place the bet.

When placing a multiple, all sections will need to win in order for you to win your bet.

The price of the multiple is formed from the original odds but is not an exact accumulation.

Multiples are handled differently depending on if each selection is taken from the same event or if each selection is from a different event. Our rules on this can be found [here](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212183509-1-11-General-Smarkets-betting-rules#:~:text=9.5%20Accumulator%20rules&text=1%20If%20one%20selection%20is,reduced%20in%20line%20with%20this.). Here are some key things to note about multiples on SBK:

**Same-game multiple: each selection is taken from the same event****Standard multiple: each selection is taken from a different event**
If one selection is voided, the whole bet will be voided.If one selection is voided, the odds will be reduced to account for the voided selection.
Selections must all be from the same event.Selections must all be from different events.
Cash out is unavailable once the event is in-play.Cash out is allowed while one or more of the events are in-play.
Not available once the event is in-play.Also available when events are in-play.
Available on football (soccer), American football, basketball and tennis (eligible markets [below](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/4412914835345-Multiples-on-SBK#h_01GX8XYNC9BJ7SR5CJW4HR4THF)).Available on any sport.
Selections from outright markets are not allowed.Selections from outright markets are not allowed.
Maximum of 10 selections in each same-game-multiple.Maximum of 10 selections in each standard multiple.

#### **Eligible markets for bet builders**

You can easily find the markets eligible for bet builders by selecting the category tab 'Bet Builder' when opening an event - only on the sports listed above.

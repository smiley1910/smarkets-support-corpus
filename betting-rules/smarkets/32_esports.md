Smarkets [general betting rules](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212183509-1-11-General-Smarkets-betting-rules) (1-11) will apply unless superseded by the [Terms and Conditions](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/213469085-Smarkets-Terms-and-Conditions) or sports specific betting rules.

## **32.1 General**

32.1 All markets are settled based on the official result of the relevant league/tournament organiser.

32.2 If a series format is changed before the series starts then all markets which were in place, prior to the time at which the format changed, will be void.

32.3 If a series format is changed after the series is turned in-play then all markets of the series will be void, except for markets which have been unconditionally determined at the point at which the series format was changed.

32.4 If a series is started and later suspended or postponed, and not resumed within 6 hours from the actual scheduled start time, then bets on all markets in respect of that series will be void, except for bets on any markets which have been unconditionally determined.

32.5 If a team is awarded a walkover by the league/tournament organiser for one or more maps in a series, any market that has not yet been unconditionally determined, including the winner market, will be voided.

32.6 If a team is renamed or rebranded, all bets on that team will stand. Smarkets reserves the right to void any bets which have been placed in relation to a team if there is material uncertainty as to the identity of that team.

32.7 Where the match finishes in a draw but the draw selection was not offered, bets will be made void.

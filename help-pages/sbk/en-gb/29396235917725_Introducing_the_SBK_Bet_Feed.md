The **Bet Feed** brings betting and community together like never before. Discover what other users are betting on, engage with their posts, build your profile, and join the conversation — all from the SBK app.

* * *

## 🔍 What is the Bet Feed?

The Bet Feed is your social betting timeline. From here, you can:

*   Explore bets placed by other members of the SBK community

*   Follow top users and copy or counter their bet

*   Like, comment, and share bets

*   Compete against others on Weekly and Monthly Leaderboards (Coming Soon)

* * *

## 🏠 Accessing the SBK Bet Feed

You can find the **Bet Feed** right on the **homepage** of the SBK app. Just tap to open and start exploring bets from across the community.

## 🧭 **Explore Bets**

Explore the Bet Feed to find bets you are interested in or to connect with other SBK users. Enhance your search by using the filters.

You can switch between:

*   **Open Bets** – See live, ongoing bets

*   **Settled Bets** – View recently settled bets

You can further filter by:

*   **Popular**, **Latest**, or **Following**

*   **Singles**, **Multiples**, **Bet Builders, Bet Builders**

## 👤 Your Profile & Privacy Settings

**Build your betting persona**

*   Add a profile picture, name, bio, and favourite team/sportsperson

*   Track your:

    *   Total bets

    *   Followers / Following

    *   Form and recent performance

*   Filter your bet history:

    *   Open

    *   Biggest Wins

    *   Close Misses

**Set your Privacy Settings**

When you first click on Bet Feed, you will be shown an explanation of what is new, plus the opportunity to set your Privacy Settings. If you did not see the onboarding flow, then you can still amend your Privacy Settings.

You can amend your privacy settings by clicking account -> Click Edit on your profile -> Scroll to Bet Feed privacy controls.

**Settings**:

*   **Public or Private Profile**

    *   Public: Other users can see your posted bets and can follow you without requesting to follow you

    *   Private: Users need to request to follow you and cannot see your posts unless they follow you

*   **Automatic Bet Sharing**

    *   On: Shares all your bets immediately after bet placement to the feed

    *   Off: No bets are shared unless you manually share the bet

## 👥 Viewing Other Users

When viewing someone else’s profile:

*   See their public details (profile picture, name, etc.)

*   Follow or unfollow them

*   Share their profile

*   See their betting form and recent bets

*   Block a user:

    *   You won’t see their posts

    *   They won’t see your posts or profile either

* * *

## ✍️ Manually Posting Tips

*   You can manually post tips from either the Bet Confirmation screen or from the My Bets page.

*   Click Share -> Click "Share bet on SBK" -> Add Caption (optional) -> Click Post

⚠️ Some words are blocked from posts and comments. If your message contains restricted terms, you’ll be asked to remove them before posting.

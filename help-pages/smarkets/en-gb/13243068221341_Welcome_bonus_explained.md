Title: Welcome bonus explained

URL Source: https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/13243068221341-Welcome-bonus-explained

Markdown Content:
New users can now enjoy 0% commission for 60 days.

**How to claim:**

1.   Enter code**COMMFREE** in the promo code field during registration.
2.   To be eligible for this offer, you need to sign up and deposit **after 00:00 BST 1st September, 2023.**
3.   Eligible customers will be able to enjoy trading with a commission rate of 0% on all bets that settle within 60 days from their first deposit.
4.   This will be applied to all bets that settle while the promotional commission rate is active on their account.
5.   Any bets which settle after the expiry of the eligibility period will settle at[standard commission rates.](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212654665-Smarkets-commission-FAQ)

## More information and full terms and conditions can be found [here](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/13242929620893-Trade-with-0-at-Smarkets-for-60-days).

If you believe you qualify for the welcome bonus but have not received it, please contact [customer support](mailto:support@smarkets.com).

Your email address is used as your login for SBK. Correspondence regarding your account will be sent to your registered email address.

On the settings page you can change your email address by following the steps below.

1. Click**My Account**at the bottom right of the page.

2. Under**Account settings**, click**Sign in details**.

![Image 2: File__4_.jpg](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/article_attachments/4409833323025/File__4_.jpg)

3. Click on the**change email address**option and fill in the required details.

Once updated, you will need to verify your new email address. We will send you an email to your updated email address containing a link to verify your email.

# Change your account password

On the settings page you can change your password by following the steps below.

1.   Load the account settings page.

2.   Once on the settings screen select [Login](https://smarkets.com/account/settings/login).

3.   Enter new password and click update. Once updated, your new password takes effect immediately.

We highly recommend setting up two-factor authentication as an additional layer of security. You can find more information [here.](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/115000822171-Using-two-factor-authentication)

_Note: Your password must contain a minimum of six characters. It must also include at least one lowercase character, one uppercase character, and one number._

# Rebuttal Map - Golf Bet Builder Void Denial (2026-08-17)

**Bet:** 4-leg accumulator, £5 @ 109/1, FedEx St. Jude Championship, all four selections = "Top 10 finish". Burns T3, Schauffele/Matsuyama/Fleetwood T7. Voided, stake returned.

**Internal decision (Michael):** UPHOLD VOID. Bet slip showed "Bet Builder"; customer placed it knowingly; Rule 9.6.1 voids a bet builder when a selection is settled as a dead heat. Customer's complaint is a retroactive attempt to reclassify the bet as a standard accumulator to convert a void into a dead heat payout.

## Customer claims vs draft treatment

1. **"A multi-selection accumulator across separate participants is not a Bet Builder."**
   - Draft: Bet slip identified it as a Bet Builder. Definition per Rule 9.6 is each selection from the same event; all four picks are markets on the same tournament. Not defined by competitor independence.
   - Status: fully addressed.

2. **"If a tournament is one event, no standard golf acca is possible, invalidating 14.3.2."**
   - Draft: 14.3.2 (dead heat rules apply) stands; 9.6.1 IS the dead heat treatment for a Bet Builder (void + stake returned). Standard golf multiple remains available across selections from different events. No invalidation.
   - Status: addressed.

3. **"14.3.2 requires a 7.2 dead heat reduction (0.80 across T7 legs = £281.60), not a void."**
   - Draft: not engaged as a reduction. For a Bet Builder the binding treatment is 9.6.1. The internal contradiction (he demands a dead heat reduction while denying the dead heat) is left to stand; we do not flag it as a gotcha in the email.
   - Status: rejected via 9.6.1, no per leg arithmetic rebuttal (not needed - whole premise of regrade is rejected).

4. **"Live chat support relied on 9.6.1 without basis."**
   - Draft: not directly rebutted; 9.6.1 is re-cited as the governing rule. Customer responsibility (Rule 2.1 + T&C s6 intro) notes he confirmed the bet type on his slip.

## Deliberately NOT responded to / not engaged

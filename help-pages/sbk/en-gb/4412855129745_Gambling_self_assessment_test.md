It is important to recognise if you have a gambling problem as early as possible so that you can put preventative measures in place and get further help if needed.

Our customers should be in full control of their gambling habits. If you think you might be at risk of developing a gambling addiction, please use the [self-assessment tool](https://smarkets.com/responsible-gambling/self-assessment/).

To speak with someone who can give you advice and support, please contact one of the organisations [listed here](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360030909971-Support-agencies-for-gambling-addiction-UK-).

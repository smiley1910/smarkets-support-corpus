# Template: SBK promotion not available to exchange user

Reusable customer-facing reply for Smarkets exchange users who ask about
SBK promotions they are not eligible for. Kind tone, positions the
exchange as the better-value product.

## Customer-facing template

Hi [name],

Thanks for getting in touch about the [promotion name].

This one isn't available to you, as SBK promotions are for SBK
customers and your account is on the Smarkets exchange. SBK is our
sportsbook, where we set the odds. The exchange is a separate product
with its own balance, and the promotion terms don't extend to it.

On the exchange you bet against other users rather than against a
bookmaker, so the odds are generally better. You can back or lay, trade
out before the event finishes, and commission is only charged on net
winnings per market, at 2% on most accounts, with nothing taken when
you lose.

If you've any questions about the exchange, just reply here.

Kind regards,
[name]
Smarkets Support

## Notes for Michael

- Optional escalation line if the customer pushes back on the decision:
  cite the specific promotion terms verbatim, e.g. "The [promotion]
  terms state: [quote the eligibility line from the offer terms]."
  Verified examples: UK welcome bonus clause 14 (eligible users "are
  not existing SBK/Smarkets Exchange users"); SB10K leaderboard clause
  22 ("users with access to Smarkets are not able to participate",
  article 36689329155869) / "Primary SBK customers only (not Smarkets
  Exchange users)" (rag-context/sbk-product.md).
- Grounding: SBK vs Smarkets are separate products with separate
  balances (confluence_draft_v2.md, "What is SBK vs Smarkets?").
- Commission sentence assumes Standard tier. Before sending, check the
  customer's tier: Standard = 2% on net winnings per market, nothing on
  losses; Pro = 1% on profit and loss per matched bet; Select = 3%;
  new customers may be on a 60-day 0% offer. Adjust or drop the
  commission sentence for non-Standard accounts.
- "Generally better" odds is the P2P mechanic: prices set by users
  rather than a house margin, and the help centre states the exchange
  results in better odds than a bookmaker. The hedge keeps it accurate.
- Voice: plain text, straight quotes, no hyphens or em dashes. Direct
  UK support register, no softeners, no appeasement lines ("nothing to
  do with your account" type reassurance is out per Michael), no
  complaints route unless the customer asks.

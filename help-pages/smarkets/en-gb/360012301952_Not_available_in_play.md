## How can we help you?

[Help Centre](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb)[Safer Gambling](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/categories/360001480391)[Contact Us](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/categories/360001480451)

For some events, our data providers are unable to provide us with live updates. Therefore, to ensure a fair trading platform, these matches will not be available to trade in-play. This will be shown at the top of the page next to the event title.

In these cases, the markets will halt at the start of the event and will not reopen. Any unmatched bet will be cancelled at this stage and no further bets can be placed once the match is in-play.

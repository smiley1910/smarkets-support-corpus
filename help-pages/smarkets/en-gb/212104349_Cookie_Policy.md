Markdown Content:
Smarkets use cookies to manage user login sessions, enhance user experience, perform essential site operations and tailor advertising and other content to reflect your specific interests.

By using Smarkets’s website and applications you agree to the use of cookies and similar technologies for the purposes we outline in this policy.

## **What is a website cookie?**

Cookies are information in the form of tiny text files that a website stores in a user's browser and can retrieve while a user is browsing the website to improve their online experience and ensure that content and functions are delivered and used more effectively.

## **What type of cookies do Smarkets track?**

**Essential**

These cookies are necessary for a website to function correctly; they enable users to navigate through the website and allow them to perform specific functions, such as placing bets, depositing and managing their account. Without these cookies, we could not provide our online service. These cookies do not allow Smarkets to access any personal data or private information from a user's device.

**Tracking**

These cookies collect information about how visitors use and interact with Smarkets. These cookies do not collect data that identifies a visitor and are only used to improve Smarkets website performance. If you do not wish to receive third-party analytics cookies, please enable your browser's ‘Do Not Track’ mode.

**Personalisation**

These cookies allow the website to provide personalised features for the user. For example, Smarkets use these cookies to show the recent markets a user has viewed. These cookies cannot track your browsing activity on other websites. Functionality cookies do not allow Smarkets to access any personal data or private information from a user's device.

**Third-party**

From time to time, Smarkets may use cookies to deliver advertising and other content to reflect your specific interests (For example those used by Google Analytics, Facebook to deliver targeted ads). Third-party vendors use cookies to serve adverts based on a user's past visits to the Smarkets website. Any data collected will be used in accordance with our own[Privacy Policy](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212816949-Privacy-Policy). If you do not wish to receive third-party analytics cookies, please enable your browser's ‘Do Not Track’ mode.

_Note: We recommend that you permit the use of cookies. If you choose to disable cookies by changing the settings in your browser then you may find that certain aspects of the Smarkets platform do not work as well as they should, and we do not accept any liability to you for any losses you may incur as a result of this._

## **18.1 General**

18.1 Tournament point and try bets are applicable to full playing time, including extra-time in matches where an official result is declared.

18.2 **Winner**: This market will be settled on which team has won after regular time has expired or if there is a draw.

18.3 **Total Score**: This market will be settled on the sum of the points scored after regular time has expired.

18.4**Half-time/full-time winners**: This market will be settled on the result at half-time and the result at end of regular time.

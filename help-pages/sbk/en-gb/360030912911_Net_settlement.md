Title: Net settlement

Smarkets

[Follow](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360030912911-Net-settlement/subscription.html "Opens a sign-in dialogue")

If you have placed multiple bets on the same market, when settlement occurs your transaction History will show the one consolidated settlement for this market.

This means you will not see an individual breakdown for all bets but your net result for that market.

In the example below, we have two bets in the winner market for the Manchester City vs Liverpool match.

**Bet Won - For Manchester City -£20 @ 2.5. Winnings =£30.**

**Bet Lost - For Liverpool - £10 @ 5.0. Losses =£10.**

To get the net settlement in the market, we need to add the winnings together and then subtract the losses. We therefore get the following calculation:

£30 - £10 = £20 profit overall.

## 🔍 What is Live Activity

Live Activity allows you to follow football matches in real time directly from your iPhone Lock Screen — no need to open the app. This feature keeps you up to date with key match details as they happen.

> ⚽️ **Note:** Live Activity is currently available **only for Football events**.

![Image 2](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/article_attachments/27345443842333)

On the Live Activity you can see:

*   Pre-match 
    *   Teams and Kits
    *   Date and time of Kick-off
    *   Access to Lineups with a single tap

*   In-play 
    *   Current time
    *   Current score
    *   Access to in-play stats with a single tap

*   Post-Match 
    *   Final Score

## 📌 How to Activate Live Activity on SBK

You can subscribe to a football event in two ways:

### From the Event Page:

1.   Open SBK and go to the football match you’re interested in

2.   Tap the **📌 Pin icon** at the top of the event

### From My Bets _(App Version 2.3.1 or later)_:

1.   Go to **My Bets**

2.   Find a bet placed on a football match

3.   Tap the **📌 Pin icon** next to the event

* * *

## ✅ Requirements

Live Activity is available **only for iOS 16.1 and above**.

> 🔧 To check your iOS version, go to **Settings > General > About > iOS Version**

* * *

## 🛑 How to Stop Live Activity

To remove the Live Activity from your Lock Screen:

*   Simply tap the **'x'** or **End** option on the activity card on your Lock Screen

*   Or unpin the event from within the SBK app by tapping the 📌 icon again

* * *

## 🧠 Troubleshooting

**Not seeing Live Activity?**

*   Make sure you're on **iOS 16.1+**

*   Check that **SBK has permission to show Live Activities**:

    *   Go to **Settings > SBK > Live Activities**

    *   Toggle the switch **ON**

*   Ensure you're only pinning **Football events**, as this feature is not yet available for other sports

Title: How to make a withdrawal

URL Source: https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212022829-How-to-make-a-withdrawal

Markdown Content:
## Follow the step-by-step guide below to help you make a withdrawal on Smarkets:

1.   Select the withdraw icon on the right-hand side of the site or [click here](https://smarkets.com/account/withdraw)to go directly to the withdrawal page.

![Image 1: Screenshot_from_2020-04-01_10-26-10.png](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/article_attachments/360007475378/Screenshot_from_2020-04-01_10-26-10.png)

2. The withdrawal page will show withdrawal options based on how deposits were previously made to your account.

![Image 2: Screenshot_from_2018-05-23_10-44-34.png](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/article_attachments/360005147791/Screenshot_from_2018-05-23_10-44-34.png)

3. Select the required withdrawal method and enter the requested details.

![Image 3: Screenshot_from_2018-05-23_10-45-15.png](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/article_attachments/360005132872/Screenshot_from_2018-05-23_10-45-15.png)

The [closed loop withdrawal policy](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212620285-Withdrawal-limits) may require you to split your withdrawal over different deposit methods. Each potential withdrawal method will show the maximum you’re allowed to withdraw.

Note: Due to UKGC regulations, users in the UK cannot cancel pending withdrawals.

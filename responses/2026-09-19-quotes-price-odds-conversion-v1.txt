The price converts to odds directly: prices are quoted in percentage basis points out of 10,000, so decimal odds = 10,000 / price. For example:

5000 = 50% = decimal odds 2.0
2500 = 25% = decimal odds 4.0
9000 = 90% = decimal odds 1.11

The quantity is separate from the odds calculation. It tells you the size of the order, in units of 1/10,000 of one unit of your currency, and represents the total pot (both sides combined) once the order is matched, so quantity 500 = £0.05 and quantity 100,000 = £10.00. If you want the back stake behind a quote, multiply the quantity by the price and divide by 100,000,000: 100,000 x 5000 / 100,000,000 = £5.00.

Both conversions are described in the price and quantity field documentation for the quotes endpoint.

SBK will from time to time offer promotions for our users to allow promo credit to be earned and then spent as free bets. To view any new, existing or past promotions you have taken part in first navigate to the promos section by clicking on promo balance the funds page or promotions on the my account tab:

![Image 2: image14.png](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/article_attachments/4644860326673/image14.png)![Image 3: image3.png](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/article_attachments/4644858514065/image3.png)

From there you will land on the promo page and any new promotions you are eligible for will display on the first screen. You can click into these to read more details, the terms of the offer and opt in:

![Image 4: image8.png](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/article_attachments/4646330267793/image8.png)![Image 5: image6.png](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/article_attachments/4646386687121/image6.png)

After opting in the card will move to the next tab along and you'll be able to track your progress from there by clicking on the promotion:

![Image 6: image18.png](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/article_attachments/4646475381905/image18.png)

Once you have qualified for a promotion or it expires the promo will move into the 'Past' column where you'll be able to confirm if you qualified or the promotion expired:

![Image 7: image20.png](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/article_attachments/4646465678481/image20.png)

For more common queries regarding promo balance and free bets please see our [FAQ.](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/4643224105105)

URL Source: https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/22786223962013-Matched-Betting-Hub

Markdown Content:
The Match Betting Hub (MBH) is your dedicated space for match betting on Smarkets. We've added three core features to the Hub;

1.   Specials
2.   Matched Betting Calculator
3.   Advanced Search and Filters

Here’s a guide to each feature and how to make the most of them.

![Image 1: Screenshot 2024-11-05 at 15.28.19.png](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/article_attachments/22786223951773)

## Specials

In our Specials section, you can easily find Boosted prices that offer Matched Bettors a positive EV.

It gives you key information such as:

*   Which Sportsbook has the offer
*   What the offer is 
    *   Selections and Boosted Price

*   Your optimum stake based on a £10 back bet
*   How much Profit you stand to win regardless of the outcome

![Image 2: Screenshot 2024-11-05 at 15.28.46.png](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/article_attachments/22786210501277)

As you can see from the first Special. If you were to place a £10 bet on Virgin Bet's Boosted price and Lay £11.27 on Smarkets, then you would guarantee yourself a return of £1.27.

![Image 3: Screenshot 2024-11-05 at 15.28.58.png](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/article_attachments/22786210504861)

If there are a lot of Specials available, you can click the See More button on the right-hand side of the Specials and then you can filter by the Sportsbook and Competition.

## Matched Betting Calculator

The _MBH Calculator_ is an essential tool for planning and calculating your bets efficiently. It allows you to:

*   **Calculate Stake Requirements** – Based on the odds and stakes involved, the calculator will give you the ideal stakes for your back and lay bets.
*   **See Potential Profit or Loss** – Instantly view the potential outcomes of your bets, helping you decide if the opportunity aligns with your strategy.
*   **Adjust Variables on the Fly** – Experiment with different odds, stakes, and commission rates to fine-tune your betting setup for the most favourable results.

You can find this on the right-hand side of the Matched Betting Hub.

![Image 4: Screenshot 2024-11-05 at 15.28.36.png](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/article_attachments/22786210506397)

For more information on Matched Betting and the use of calculators, you can read our other help centre article [here](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/115000350131-How-to-calculate-matched-betting-bets)

## Advanced Search and Filters

With the _Advanced Search & Filters_, finding the right match or market is easier than ever. Here’s how to use it:

*   **Filter by Sport** – Focus on the sports you prefer or feel most confident in.
*   **Odds Ranges** – Narrow your search by specific odds ranges to find markets that meet your requirements.
*   **Event Type** – Search for events like “football”, “tennis”, or “basketball” to quickly access the sports markets you’re interested in.
*   **Date and Time** – Plan ahead by filtering events based on when they’re scheduled, so you can maximize your betting timeline.

![Image 5: Screenshot 2024-11-05 at 15.46.23.png](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/article_attachments/22786223958557)

The _Advanced Search & Filters_ help you sift through the available matches and markets quickly, focusing only on the bets that align with your strategies and preferences.

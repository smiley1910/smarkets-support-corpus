## **28.1 General**

28.1  If a match starts but is not completed, the player progressing to the next round will be deemed the winner, or in the final, the player that is awarded the title will be declared the winner.

28.2  Correct score markets will be settled on the correct score at the end of the match. If the match starts, but is not completed, correct score bets will be made void.

28.3 For bets on outright markets, if a player does not take part in the tournament all bets on that player will be voided.

28.4 If a game ends in a draw, all bets will be voided except for markets where these outcomes are specifically listed or there is an 'any other outcome' selection in the market.

# Bet result email updates

Smarkets send you updates of your betting via email. On the [settings](https://smarkets.com/account/settings/general) page you can can change the frequency based on the following three options:

*   Every time a market you’ve bet on settles.
*   A maximum of one email per day.
*   Not receive any emails in relation to your bets.

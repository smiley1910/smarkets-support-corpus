1. 18+; UK and Ireland customers; verified SBK accounts; opt-in required.

2. One account per person; standard SBK account & promotional terms apply.

3. Original Post: A bet you publish in the SBK Bet Feed.

4. Copy Bet: A bet created by another customer via the Copy action from your Original Post.

5. Qualifying (Settled) Copy Bet: A Copy Bet that is placed and subsequently settled with a valid result.

6. You earn £/€ 1 Free Bet for every 5 Qualifying (Settled) Copy Bets across your Original Posts.

7. Offer expires on 31st December 2026.

8. Bets copied prior to opting in do not count towards the promotion.

9. Counting rule: Maximum one Qualifying Copy Bet per person per Original Post (duplicates/rewrites/edits by the same person on the same post do not add further counts).

10. Copies are tallied continuously; rewards are issued each time your tally hits a multiple of 5.

11. Progress carries over between issuance points.

12. The maximum reward amount participant can earn within one week is £/€ 20.

13. A Copy Bet must be placed and settled to qualify.

14. Voids, cancellations, pushes, and fully refunded wagers do not qualify.

15. Self-copies (you copying your own post) do not qualify.

16. SBK may exclude Copy Bets arising from obvious error, palpable error, or technical malfunction.

17. Free Bets are auto-credited on a weekly basis on Tuesdays before 20:00 GMT.

18. The number of Free Bets a user can earn is capped at £20.

19. Free Bet face value: £/€ 1; stake not returned; 7-day expiry from credit.

20. Minimum odds to use a Free Bet: 1.8 (4/5).

21. Free Bets are non-withdrawable, non-transferable, and cannot be exchanged for cash.

22. We may withhold, remove, or reclaim rewards in cases of suspected abuse, collusion, duplicate accounts/devices, automation, or fraud (including attempts to manufacture copies).

23. SBK’s decision is final. We may request additional verification.

24. SBK may amend, suspend, or end this promotion where necessary for regulatory, integrity, or operational reasons.

25. Responsible play applies.

# Pending bets

Bets placed in markets managed by [Instant Match](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212106249-Instant-Match) will be subject to a ‘pending’ confirmation delay. This system allows customers to be matched at the current price whilst also protecting them from court siding and faster feeds.

The [length of the delay](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/en-gb/articles/212106249-Instant-Match) is determined by the competition the event takes place in.

If a material event occurs within this pending period, the bet will be made void.

Once the delay has elapsed a new entry will be made into the account page to confirm the status of the bet (as displayed below).

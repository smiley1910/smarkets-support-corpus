If you have forgotten your password, you can request a new password by following the forgotten password procedure.

1.   On the login page, click ‘Forgot password?’. This will redirect you to the password reset page.
![Image 2: File__3_.jpg](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/article_attachments/4409833185169/File__3_.jpg)

2.   Once on the password reset page, you will need to enter your email address and date of birth.
![Image 3: IMG_1250.PNG](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/article_attachments/4409833195153/IMG_1250.PNG)

3.   Instructions on how to reset your password will then be sent to you via your SBK registered email address._At the bottom of this email thread, there's also a link to reset your password which is in a green box. Kindly click on it and follow the steps to reset your password._

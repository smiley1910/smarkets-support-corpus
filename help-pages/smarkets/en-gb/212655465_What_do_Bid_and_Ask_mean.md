# What do 'Bid’ and 'Ask' mean?

“Bid” or "Ask" are displayed when there are no current users offering bets into the market for you to match with on the back or lay side of the market respectively.

![Image 3: image__3_.png](https://help.smarkets.com/hc/article_attachments/360024258092/image__3_.png)

You can wait for someone else to offer odds into the market or you can place your own bet into the market. In these circumstances we recommend checking how other operators are pricing the event to ensure you do not place a bet that is out of line.

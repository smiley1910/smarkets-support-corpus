If your bet has been voided or cancelled your stake is returned, so you incur no loss.

## Cancelled bets

Bets are cancelled when the market is halted prior to bet placement confirmation. The market will halt at the start and the end of the event, as well as when a material event occurs in scouted football markets.

In horse racing, the market will halt whenever a non-runner is removed from the race.

## Voided bets

SBK will void any bets where the outcome cannot be reasonably determined. Common reasons for the voiding of bets include:

*   Postponement/cancellation of an event.
*   A horse being declared a non-runner in non-ante post horse market.
*   Bets placed after the outcome has already been determined.

Please also refer to our [sports specific betting rules](https://help.getsbk.com/hc/en-gb/sections/360005958351-Sport-specific-rules) for other situations which may result in bets being voided.

_Note: Markets will only be voided due to postponement/cancellation if the event is not concluded within 36 hours of the originally scheduled start time._
